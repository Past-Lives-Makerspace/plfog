Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information {{ brand_name }} collects through its member app and website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain. If anything is unclear, email us at {{ brand_support_email }}.

Who This Covers

This app is the member hub for {{ brand_name }}, a community workshop in Portland, Oregon. The mobile app shows the same website you use in a browser, so this policy applies whether you use the app or the site.

What We Collect

  • Account. The email address you sign in with (and any extra addresses you add). We sign you in with a one-time code sent to your email, so we do not store a password.
  • Profile you choose to share. Your name, phone number, Discord handle, pronouns, photo, and a short about-you. You control what other members can see with a per-field public or hidden toggle on each item.
  • Membership and billing. Your membership status and plan. Payments are processed by Stripe; your card details go directly to Stripe and are not stored on our servers.
  • Things kept private from other members. Your legal name, billing details, and emergency contact are used only for running the makerspace and are never shown on your member card.
  • Communications. Emails we send you (sign-in codes, announcements, class details) and, if you opt in, our newsletter.
  • Notifications. If you turn on push notifications, we store the subscription your device needs to receive them.
  • Usage and device. Basic technical data such as your IP address, browser or device type, and pages viewed, through standard server logs and analytics.

How We Use It

  • To sign you in and run your membership.
  • To show the member directory, guilds, classes, calendar, and announcements.
  • To process payments and send receipts.
  • To send messages you have asked for or that are needed to run the space.
  • To keep the app secure and working, and to understand how it is used.

When We Share It

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • Stripe — payments and billing.
  • Mailchimp — our opt-in newsletter.
  • Google Analytics — anonymous, aggregate usage.
  • Discord — only if you choose to link your Discord account.
  • Google Calendar — when you book a facility tour with a Tour Guide who has connected their Google Calendar, the tour is added to their calendar with your name, party size, email and phone, so they know who is coming.
  • Email and hosting providers — to deliver messages and serve the app.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our members and staff.

Google Calendar for Tour Guides

Tour Guides can choose to connect their own Google Calendar on the My Hours tab of the Tours page. No one else is asked for access to their Google account.

  • What we access. The events on calendars you own, and the list of those calendars. Nothing else in your Google account.
  • How we use it. We add each tour you give to the calendar you pick, change it when the tour moves, and remove it when the tour is cancelled. We read when you are busy so visitors are not offered a tour at those times, leaving out the tours we added.
  • What we keep. The Google account you connected, the names of your calendars for the picker, and the start and end of your busy times. We do not keep the titles, descriptions or guests of your own events. Google's access tokens are encrypted on our servers.
  • What we never do. We do not sell your Google data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.
  • Turning it off. Disconnect on the My Hours tab revokes our access at Google and deletes what we stored. You can also remove our access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

{{ brand_name }}'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your Choices

  • Change what other members see anytime with the public or hidden toggles in your profile settings.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter using the link in any newsletter email.
  • Turn push notifications on or off in your settings.
  • Ask us to view, correct, or delete your information by emailing {{ brand_support_email }}.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your information while you are a member and for a reasonable period afterward for records and legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including passwordless sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Children

The app is intended for makerspace members. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we update this policy we will change the date above, and we will let members know if the changes are significant.
