Give Tours

Show curious people around Past Lives on hours you choose. The app handles the booking, and when someone you toured becomes a member, you're paid $50.

{% whats_new_button "tours.give" %} {% if is_guide %} Go to Tours {% else %} {% endif %}

What You Get

Hours You Set No fixed schedule. Give tours every week, or only on the dates you pick, and add Days Off when you are away.
Show Off the Space Walk curious people through the place you love, and help them picture themselves making things here.
$50 per New Member When a visitor you gave a tour to becomes a member, you're paid $50.
The App Does the Rest Visitors book online or at the lobby kiosk and get their confirmation and a reminder email. You get an email, a bell and a push for each booking, and a ping when they check in.
Your Calendar, Kept Current Connect your Google Calendar if you use one. Your tours land on it, and times it shows you busy are not offered to visitors.
A Team of Guides The Calendar tab shows every guide's hours and the days nobody covers yet, so you can fill the gaps. Each tour has Notes the guides share.

How It Works

  1. Take the tutorial. Five short steps, right on this page. The last one has Become a Tour Guide.
  2. Set your hours. On My Hours, add the times you can give tours, every week or on dates you pick.
  3. Visitors book you. They pick an open time on tours.pastlives.space or at the lobby kiosk, and you get an email, a bell and a push.
  4. Show them around. Meet them in the lobby, give the tour, then mark it Attended on the Past tab.

Getting Paid

$50 Per New Member When a visitor you gave a tour to becomes a member, you're paid $50.
Attended Your Record Mark each tour Attended on the Past tab. That is the record of who you toured.
Tours Given Your Count Once you're set up, your card on the Tours page counts your tours and visitors.

What We Ask Of You

  • Set hours you can keep, and add Days Off when you will be away.
  • Be in the lobby to meet your visitors when the tour starts.
  • If you pick Confirm First, answer each request by 24 hours before the tour.
  • After each tour, mark it Attended or No Show on the Past tab.

Common Questions

Q Is there a fixed schedule?

No. Visitors can only book times inside the hours you set, and you can change them any time on My Hours. Add Days Off when you are away.

Q How many visitors come on one tour?

Up to the seats you set for those hours, anywhere from 1 to 50.

Q What if I can't make a tour?

On the Upcoming tab, press the ... on the tour. Edit Guide or Time moves it or hands it to another guide, and Cancel Tour cancels it. The visitor is emailed, with your note if you add one.

Q Do I need a Google Calendar?

No, it is optional. If you connect one, your tours land on it, and times it shows you busy are not offered to visitors.

Q Can I stop giving tours?

Yes. Delete your hours on My Hours and visitors can no longer book you. To stop being a Tour Guide altogether, ask an admin.

Step 1 of 5

Setting Up

  • On My Hours, set the times you can give tours: Every Week for hours that repeat, or Available On for dates you pick.
  • Each window is carved into back to back tours of the length you choose, each with 1 to 50 seats.
  • Add Days Off for when you are away. Your hours offer nothing on those days, and tours already booked stay booked.
  • Connect your Google Calendar on My Hours if you use one. Your tours land on it, and times it shows you busy are not offered to visitors.
  • The Calendar tab shows every guide's hours. Days With No Tour Hours lists the days nobody covers yet, and Add My Hours fills one.

Step 2 of 5

When Someone Books

  • Visitors book an open time inside your hours online at tours.pastlives.space, or at the kiosk in the lobby.
  • You get an email, a bell and a push for each booking, and again when a visitor moves or cancels.
  • To confirm or decline each booking yourself, pick Confirm First under Confirming Bookings on My Hours.
  • Reschedule or cancel from the Upcoming tab: press the ... on the tour for Edit Guide or Time, or Cancel Tour. The visitor is emailed, with your note if you add one.

Step 3 of 5

A Tour Day

  • Your visitors get a reminder email 24 hours before the tour.
  • Meet them in the lobby. When they check in at the kiosk you get a bell and a push: "Your 10:00 tour is here."
  • Afterwards, mark the tour Attended or No Show on the Past tab.

Step 4 of 5

The Lobby Kiosk

  • The screen in the lobby has two buttons, I Booked a Tour and Schedule a Tour.
  • Visitors check in with the email they booked with or their name, and the kiosk tells them you will be right with them.
  • Walk ins can book an open time right there.

Step 5 of 5

Getting Paid

  • When a visitor you gave a tour to becomes a member, you're paid $50.
  • Mark each tour Attended on the Past tab: that is the record of who you toured.
  • Want to stop later? Delete your hours on My Hours, or ask an admin to turn Tour Guide off.
