It is member free text shown to every reader, and a renderer buys nothing here but an XSS surface. expand_over is the PHONE's capacity, not the desktop's, and deliberately so. The clamp is a CSS fact the server cannot measure, so this is a guess either way — and the two errors are not equal. Guess too high and a 150-character report is clamped on a 390px screen with no expander at all, so the tail is unreachable on the device where reporting actually happens. Guess too low and a rare short-but-over-threshold reason shows a button that does nothing when tapped. The second error is much the cheaper one, so the threshold is the narrow width's three lines (about 120 characters at 0.9rem italic in a card), not the desktop's. Parameters: reason — required. clamp_class — optional, e.g. pl-wp-mod__quote--short for the 2-line clamp. expand_over — optional, the character count above which the expander renders.
