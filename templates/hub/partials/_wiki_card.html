Parameters: page — required. The object. A duck-typed stand-in works: spec E passes a GovernanceDocument and only touches title, get_absolute_url, status_pill. snippet — optional. This parameter is the whole reason search results do not need a card of their own. source_chip — optional. A quiet neutral label ("Wiki", "Help", "Policies"). Not a pill, not colored. Omitted when the surface has only one source. actions_partial — optional. A template path rendered in .pl-wp-card__actions at the right of the row. This is where spec B's _wiki_verify_control.html with compact=True lives; without the slot B has nowhere to put a Verify button but a second card. Exactly one status pill per card (the brief's "no pill salad"): kind, guild and equipment are quiet muted text and never colored. The action slot sits OUTSIDE the title link so a tap on it is not swallowed by the card's own link. {% endcomment %}
