{% comment %} The Subscribe menu: the makerspace's Member and Public Google calendars, each offered in the two forms a calendar app can take, grouped by app because that is the choice a member makes. Apple Calendar (and Outlook) open a webcal:// link as a live subscription. Android has no handler for webcal://, in Chrome or in the app, so Google Calendar gets its own add link, a plain https URL that the native shells hand to the system. An event is pushed to exactly one of the two calendars, so the note says to take both for everything.
