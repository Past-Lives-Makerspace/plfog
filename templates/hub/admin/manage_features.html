Manage Features

What shows up in the sidebar. Every feature below is On for normal operation. Coming soon leaves the entry visible but not clickable, showing your message when someone hovers or tabs onto it; Hidden removes the entry altogether. These switches are cosmetic, except My Tab. They change the sidebar and nothing else: every page stays reachable by its own link, for everyone, in all three states. Use them to tidy the sidebar or to say a feature is on its way, not to keep anyone out of something. My Tab is the one exception: Coming soon and Hidden both switch tab billing off, as its card says.
