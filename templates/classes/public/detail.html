Questions

What's your cancellation policy?
Cancel up to 7 days before the first session for a full refund. Within 7 days, we can usually transfer your spot to a future class or to someone on the waitlist.
Is the space accessible?
Yes — our studio has step-free entry, accessible restrooms, and adjustable-height workstations available on request. Email info@pastlives.space with any specific needs and we'll make it work.
What if I've never done this before?
Most of our classes are designed for beginners and welcome curious newcomers. Check the Prerequisites section above for any required experience.
