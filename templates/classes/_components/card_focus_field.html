{% comment %} The catalog card preview and focal point tool (design spec §B.3, the whole card since the CMS follow ups spec §D): the right hand pane of the composer's "Your Photo, Two Shapes" section. Renders the REAL catalog card (classes/public/_class_card.html, through the genuine CatalogGroup in `card_group`) inside two card shaped frames, one at the width the catalog packs on a laptop and one at the width it packs on a phone, both driven live by two sliders. Photo, title, instructor, dates, price and spots: exactly what members see, so nobody mistakes the pane for a photo cropper. Three states: saved with a photo of its own (offering.hero_image_url, uploaded or imported) both frames render the real card with the live position and the live title; the sliders are shown. saved with no photo both frames still render the real card, with the category or logo placeholder the catalog would show, and the sliders are hidden until a photo exists. The hero field uploads instantly on a saved class and rewrites #hero-preview, which cardFocus.localSrc mirrors: the moment that happens the placeholder frames give way to the real card rendering the new photo (live_src) and the sliders appear, no reload needed. unsaved (create mode, no card_group) a photo picked before the first save exists only as the hero field's local data URL, mirrored by cardFocus.localSrc into a skeleton card: the media strip plus a body with the live title and one muted line. The sliders show once a photo is picked. Expects `form` (with the hidden card_focus field from _CardFocusMixin), `offering` (may be unsaved) and `card_group` (None until the class has a pk). `liveTitle` lives on the composer root's x-data. Both card frames sit inside .cp-page, so the .cls-card picks up the catalog's real border, radius, and overflow, and .cls-media supplies the 150px height. The two widths are derived in the CSS comment above .pl-card-focus__frame--laptop. {% endcomment %}
