{% comment %} The Instructors control on the composer's Basics step (#686): the people listed, one checked row each (the announcement composer's "Added people" rows), and an "Add an instructor" select that appends a row. Unticking a row removes that person; the last row cannot be unticked; the select resets after each add, drops the person just added and offers back anyone unticked. Alpine only: the rows live in the browser until the save posts them as name="instructors", in the order listed. A viewer who may not change the list (the guild's staff on a draft they do not teach) gets no control at all: the form built for them carries neither field, so nothing they post changes it.
