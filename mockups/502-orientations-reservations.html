Instructor

Instructor
Gives a public instructor page and the ability to create classes. Turning it off removes both (existing classes are untouched).

Admin capabilities

Site-wide duties. Each one both sends this member the matching approval or alert emails and lets them act on it, without granting full admin. Per-guild lead and staff roles are managed on each guild's own Staff tab, not here.

CMS Administrator
Approves and publishes classes for every guild. Gets the class-review emails, and the requests from members who want to host a class.
Space & Cubby Administrator
Reviews space and cubby requests, and gets those request emails.
Discount Code Administrator
Approves discount codes, and gets discount-request emails.
Calendar Administrator
Reviews Calendar and meeting proposals, and gets those emails.
Billing Administrator
Sees the admin Payments dashboard and gets an alert when a member's automatic payment fails.
Refunds
Sends Stripe refunds for class and orientation payments. Adds Refund buttons to payment pages that are already reachable, and opens no new pages, so pair it with Billing Administrator for the Payments panel.
Equipment Administrator
Adds new equipment and manages every tool site-wide, including its details, staff, orientations, and hours.
Space Manager
Adds rooms and spaces members can reserve, such as the loading dock, and manages the ones they add. Cannot add tools.
