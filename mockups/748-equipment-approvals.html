Needs Approval is the first card on the tab (the Orientation tab puts Pending Requests first for the same reason). It renders while Needs approval is on, or while any row still waits after the switch is turned off; it is gone otherwise, so a free instant item's tab is unchanged. Approve posts with no confirm and answers a toast, "Approved. Jane has been emailed."; the row moves to Upcoming. Rows sort soonest first, unpaginated (they are few by nature). A held-for-payment row shows in Upcoming with its release time and no Cancel. A manager cancel of a paid confirmed row now also says "Their $50.00 is refunded in full."

Status pills: Awaiting payment (neutral), Awaiting approval (warn: someone has to act), Confirmed (ok), Declined (neutral), Cancelled (neutral); a "Paid $X" neutral pill beside any paid row, "Refunded" after a decline or cancel. The Needs Approval chip shows for staff only and only while the count is above 0; its empty state, reached by a stale link, is "Nothing is waiting for approval." Member view: own awaiting payment rows get "Pay Now" in the menu; own awaiting approval rows keep "Cancel Reservation". The Status filter gains the new statuses on its own.
