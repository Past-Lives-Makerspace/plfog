## Email Model — Three Stores (IMPORTANT)

Three places an email can live for a Member. Future agents MUST understand which is authoritative when. See `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-04-07-user-email-aliases-design.md` for the full rationale.

| Store | Role |
|---|---|
| `Member._pre_signup_email` (DB column `email`, accessed via `db_column="email"`) | Source of truth ONLY when `Member.user` is None (Airtable-imported members who haven't signed up yet). |
| `allauth.account.EmailAddress` | Source of truth for any Member with a linked User. Owns login, verification, and the primary flag. |
| `User.email` | Mirror kept in sync by allauth. Never read or write directly from app code. |

### Reading "the" email
- Use `member.primary_email` (property). It returns the live value: primary `EmailAddress.email` for linked members, `_pre_signup_email` otherwise, with a final fallback to `user.email`.
- **Exception:** `airtable_sync/` reads `_pre_signup_email` directly because Airtable is the external source of truth for unlinked members and we don't want sync to re-enter allauth.

### Writing (user-facing)
- Members manage their own emails at `/accounts/email/` (themed `templates/account/email.html` over allauth's built-in `account_email` view).
- Admin: the `MemberEmailInline` is **only** shown for unlinked members. Once linked, it's hidden because allauth.EmailAddress is now the truth.

### Login
- Allauth login-by-code looks up any verified `EmailAddress` row, so any verified alias works automatically.
- Pre-signup aliases imported into `MemberEmail` are promoted to `EmailAddress` when the user signs up — handled by `MemberEmail.objects.migrate_to_user(user)`, called from the `ensure_user_has_member` signal.

## Airtable Sync

`Member`, `Space`, and `Lease` are **read from Airtable** via `airtable_pull` management command. They do NOT sync back — treat as read-only from Django's perspective. `airtable_record_id` fields link to Airtable rows.

`VotePreference` and `FundingSnapshot` sync **to Airtable** on save (outbound only).

## Fog Roles → Django Permissions

`Member.set_fog_role()` calls `sync_user_permissions()` which sets:
- `admin` → `is_superuser=True`, `is_staff=True`
- `guild_officer` → `is_superuser=False`, `is_staff=True`
- `member` → both False

Never set `is_staff`/`is_superuser` directly — always go through `set_fog_role()`.

## Guild Leads — edit authority

A guild's lead is **only** the `Guild.guild_lead` FK (→ Member). That FK alone grants edit rights — no FOG role, Django group, or `is_staff` flag required. `fog_role` (admin / guild_officer) is a *separate*, cross-guild staff tier and keeps working independently.

- **One source of truth for "who may edit what":** `membership/permissions.py` — `can_edit_guild(request, guild)`, `can_manage_cubbies(request, guild)` (a guild's cubbies, #1015: its lead and staff, and admins; the Guild Officer tier opens nothing, and `cubby_guilds` is its queryset twin), `can_edit_class(request, offering)`, `can_edit_category(request, category)` (all `view_as`-aware). For role-based checks (commands, model logic, tests) use `Member.can_edit_guild` / `Member.can_edit_class` and `ClassOffering.objects.editable_by(member)`. Don't reimplement these inline in views.
- Never gate a guild-lead surface on `is_staff`, `fog_role`, or `member_type` — that reintroduces the drift that left real leads unable to save. `MemberType.GUILD_LEAD` is an Airtable label only and grants nothing.
- Guild was removed from the Django admin (v1.6.0). Assign a lead with `manage.py set_guild_lead --guild <name|id> --member <email>` (it warns if that member has no linked user). Detect drift with `manage.py audit_guild_leads` (leads with no login, inactive leads, guilds with no lead).

### Guild Staff (co-leads, secretaries, treasurers, orientors)

Beyond the single `guild_lead` FK, a guild has `GuildStaffMembership` rows (`role` ∈ co_lead / secretary / treasurer / orienter), managed by leads/staff on the **Staff tab** of the guild edit page. **Every staff role grants the same authority as the lead, except class review** — `can_edit_guild`/`can_edit_class`/`can_manage_orientations`/`can_manage_cubbies` all treat staff like the lead, and `editable_by` includes staffed guilds. Lead-facing orientation requests fan out to `Guild.leadership_members()` (lead + all staff, deduped). Class review (#816) belongs to the lead and the staff marked `can_review_classes` (Class Reviewer, set per member on the Staff tab by the lead or an admin through `Guild.set_class_reviewer`, gate `permissions.can_set_class_reviewers`): `Guild.class_reviewers()` (every class in the guild, a reviewer's own classes included, #883) drives the `class_review_requested` resolver and Remind lead, `Member.class_reviewed_guilds` drives `awaiting_guild_lead`, `Guild.reviews_classes` drives `ClassApproval.may_be_decided_by` on the tokenized review page. The former orientation-only `Guild.orienters` M2M was folded into the `orienter` staff role (migration `0049`); orientors now get full lead permissions. Use `Member.is_guild_staff` / `Member.staffed_guilds` and `Guild.is_staffed_by` / `staff_by_role` / `leadership_members`.

**Two doors, one set of methods (#968).** The guild Staff tab and the member edit page's Permissions > Guild Roles (admin only, `templates/hub/admin/_guild_roles.html`) change the same rows: `Guild.add_staff` (a role, a title, or Class Reviewer alone, which adds a row titled "Class Reviewer" or switches an existing staff member on), `Guild.remove_staff` (retires orienter hours on the last row), `GuildStaffMembership.retitle`, `Guild.assign_lead` / `Guild.clear_lead`, and the Staff tab's own `hub_guild_staff_class_reviewer` switch. `GuildStaffMembership.title_problem` is the one validator both pages' forms call. `Member.guild_roles()` is the person side's list. Never write a staff row inline in a view.

**Vocabulary (keep it straight in copy and code):** *lead* = the single `Guild.guild_lead` holder; *staff* = a `GuildStaffMembership` role holder; *guild leadership* = lead + staff together (the term the resolvers use). These are **per-guild**. Never call any of them a "Guild Administrator" — "Administrator" is reserved for the site-wide Admin Capabilities (CMS / Space & Cubby / Discount Code / Calendar / Billing Administrator, plus the action-only Refunds grant). A surface whose audience is lead + staff should say "lead or staff," not "lead" (that undersold who can act and hid a real authorization bug on the orientations dashboard).

### Renting a cubby (#978)

`CubbyRental` (cubby and member PROTECT; status Awaiting Payment, Active, Past Due, Ended) is one member's monthly Stripe subscription for one cubby; a member may rent any number. `uq_cubbyrental_live_cubby` allows one live rental (awaiting payment, active, past due) per cubby, and `Cubby.state` reads Rented from it (`CubbyQuerySet.with_renter` annotates the renter for a whole shelf in one query). `CubbyPayment` is one row per paid Stripe invoice (`uq_cubbypayment_invoice`), with the guild's `cubby_payee` frozen when it landed (its share is paid through a `Payout`, #979) and `stripe_refund_id` when it was refunded.

`membership/cubby_rentals.py` is the only writer of a rental's status. `start_rental` holds the cubby under the guild lock (part 1's) and the cubby row lock, checks the floor as it is now, and opens a `mode="subscription"` Checkout with an inline price and product (`kind=cubby_rental`, `rental_pk`, `cubby_pk`, `member_pk` on the session and the subscription, key `cubby-checkout-<pk>`, 1 hour); the customer is the member's Tab customer. A member's own hold resumes its open Checkout. `settle_subscription` is the one mirror: every webhook (`checkout.session.completed`, `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `customer.subscription.updated` and `.deleted`), the return page and the sweep re-read the subscription from Stripe and copy status, amount, item, product, period end and cancel at period end (or `cancel_at`) onto the row under its lock, so order never matters; Ended is final, and `unpaid` cancels the subscription. A billing subscription's card is moved to the customer's default and cleared from the subscription, so the Billing Portal's Update Payment Method pays the next charge. A paid Checkout whose hold is gone takes the cubby back if it is still free; otherwise the rental is made Ended, the subscription cancelled and its first payment refunded in full (`cubby-refund-<invoice>`), and the member told. Raising the floor never changes an existing amount; Change Amount follows the new floor, except keeping the current amount. Change Amount reprices the item on its product with `proration_behavior="none"`. Holds are deleted, never ended, by `checkout.session.expired`, the cancel page and the `release_cubby_payment_holds` sweep (every 15 minutes, holds over two hours, Stripe asked first). Archiving a cubby or its shelf, or putting it out of service, is refused while it is rented or held. Deleting an account cancels its rentals at once with no refund (`end_for_deleted_account`). Events `cubbies.rented`, `cubbies.payment_failed` (once per invoice), `cubbies.rental_ended` and `cubbies.rental_refunded` go to the renter, bell on and email forced, from `membership/emails/cubby_*`.

**Staff (#979).** `CubbyRental.source` is Stripe (a member's own subscription) or, for a rental guild staff assigned from the Rentals tab, Comped (amount 0, the only rental `ck_cubbyrental_amount` lets cost nothing) or Paid Another Way (what the member pays the guild directly, $1 to $500, never charged here). `assign_rental` takes the same guild and cubby locks as renting and refuses a cubby that is rented, held, archived or out of service (`assign_problem`; the Cubbies tab switch does not matter); the rental is Active from today with no subscription. `end_rental` (End Rental, and a member's Cancel Rental on an assigned rental) ends a live rental today with no refund: a Stripe rental's subscription is cancelled now (`prorate=false`, key `cubby-end-<pk>`) while its row is locked, so the `customer.subscription.deleted` that follows finds it Ended and adds nothing; an assigned one makes no Stripe call. `end_reason` is what staff said, emailed to the member. The guild's lead and staff hear `cubbies.rental_started` (bell on, email off; an assigning staff member is left out), `cubbies.rental_past_due` (its email in the 8 AM digest, whose still-open check drops a rental no longer Past Due, period `cubby_past_due:<pk>:<invoice>`) and `cubbies.staff_rental_ended` ("Clear A3's shelf", bell and email, whenever a started rental ends, a deleted account's included), all copy mode from `core/events/copy.py`. The Rentals tab reads `cubbies.RentalsSummary`, `staff_rentals` and `RentalRow`; `membership/cubby_exports.rental_row` is the CSV's flat row, the shape a later `rental_to_airtable` would send. A paid invoice's guild share is paid to its frozen payee by `billing.payouts` (`billing/AGENTS.md`).

**Waitlist (#980).** `CubbyWaitlistEntry` (guild, member, joined_at, notified_at, notified_cubby, left_at) is one member waiting for any cubby in a guild; `uq_cubbywait_open` allows one waiting entry (not notified, not left) per member per guild, so leaving and joining again puts them at the back. `membership/cubby_waitlist.py` is its only writer: `join` (under the guild lock; refused while a cubby is open, the tab is off or the guild has no cubbies; a member who rents here may join), `leave`, `remove` (staff, the member is not told), `leave_everywhere` (account deletion, run first in `end_for_deleted_account`), `place_in_line`, `line` and `waiting_count` (live accounts only, the same filter `notify_next` picks by, so a deactivated member never holds a place) and `is_full` (rented, held, out of service and archived all count as taken). Every change that opens a cubby calls `open_for_waitlist(cubby)` inside it: `_mirror` ending a rental (the webhook, unpaid), `end_rental` (End Rental, a member's cancel of an assigned rental), `end_for_deleted_account` (its end is guarded on a live status, so the webhook for its own cancel and the deletion open the cubby once), `_delete_hold` (expired webhook, cancel page, sweep, a failed rent; not the member's own hold replaced inside `start_rental`, which opens only when the new rent is then refused), `save_cubby` (a new cubby, or Out of Service off), `restore_cubby`, `restore_unit`, and the Cubbies tab switch coming on (`open_all_for_waitlist`). Resizing never opens one. `open_for_waitlist` registers a robust `on_commit` callback, so a rolled back change tells nobody and a failure to tell never fails the change; `notify_next` then takes the guild lock, re-checks the cubby is still open and the tab on, claims the oldest waiting entry of a live account (not deleted, user still active) with a conditional update on its still waiting row, passing to the next when it left meanwhile (never `select_for_update().first()`, which under LIMIT can return nothing once the locked row stops matching), and emits `cubbies.waitlist_opened` (bell on, email forced, period `cubby_waitlist:<entry pk>:opened`, `membership/emails/cubby_waitlist_opened.*`). The lock is why two cubbies opening at once tell two different members (`tests/membership/cubby_waitlist_race_spec.py`), and a retried webhook tells nobody new because the callers call only on the change itself. Nothing is scheduled.

Stripe setup Felix owns: the four subscription events on the webhook endpoint, the Billing Portal's default configuration saved, and failed payments set to cancel the subscription after the last retry.

### Who gets paid for cubbies (#977)

`Guild.cubby_payee` is one of the guild's lead and staff (`Guild.cubby_payee_choices`), or nobody, which keeps the whole payment with Past Lives. `remove_staff`, `assign_lead` and `clear_lead` call `drop_cubby_payee_unless_leader`, so a payee who no longer leads or staffs the guild is cleared; one who still holds another staff row there stays.

## Admin Capabilities

`AdminCapability` grants a member a **scoped admin duty** without promoting them to the full `fog_role=admin` tier. Each capability does two things at once: it **routes** the matching approval/alert notifications to the holder, and it **grants the action** (the holder can actually approve/decline that object type).

| Capability (`AdminCapability.Capability`) | Routes these notifications | Grants this action |
|---|---|---|
| `CLASS_APPROVER` (CMS Administrator) | `class_review_requested` (lead-less categories), `class_validation_requested` | approve/validate classes |
| `SPACE_APPROVER` (Space & Cubby Administrator) | `space.lease_requested`, `space.cubby_requested` | review space requests (`hub._map_reviewer_scope` grants admin-level review) |
| `DISCOUNT_APPROVER` (Discount Code Administrator) | `discount_code.requested` | approve any discount code (`DiscountCode.approver_for` → `approves_any`) |
| `EVENTS_APPROVER` (Calendar Administrator) | `event.submitted`, `meeting.item_proposed` (site-wide/council) | review calendar proposals (`hub._reviewer_guild_scope` grants admin-level review); open Manage Events (`events_admin_access_required`, #831) with its Duplicate and Unpublish; edit, delete and retry sync on site-wide events (`can_administer_events`) |
| `BILLING_APPROVER` (Billing Administrator) | `billing.charge_failed_admin`, `refund_failed`, `billing.late_fee_orphan_payment`, `membership.orientation_orphan_payment` | sees the admin Payments dashboard (`billing_admin_access_required`) |
| `REFUNDS` (Refunds) | (action-only — routes nothing) | issue/retry Stripe refunds (`refund_authority_required`); opens no new pages on its own |
| `EQUIPMENT` (Equipment Administrator) | only the fallback of `orientation_requested`, `equipment.reservation_made` and `equipment.reservation_needs_approval` (#748) for equipment with no managers and no guild leadership (#746); a holder hears nothing about equipment someone else runs | create equipment of every kind and manage all of it site-wide (`can_create_equipment` / `can_manage_equipment` site tier) |
| `SPACE_MANAGER` (Space Manager) | (action-only — routes nothing) | create rooms and spaces only (`creatable_equipment_kinds`, #502); manages only what they add, through an `EquipmentStaffMembership` row the add view writes |
| `EVENT_CREATOR` (Can create events) | (action-only — routes nothing) | open the Calendar's event composer (`/events/add/`, the same "+ Add event" button an admin sees) whatever `member_event_policy` says; every event they add is review-first (`permissions.event_create_mode` → `REVIEW`): it enters the existing review queue and an admin or Calendar Administrator's approval announces it. Admins and `EVENTS_APPROVER` holders publish directly (`DIRECT`) and may edit, delete and retry sync on site-wide events (`can_administer_events`). |
| `WEBMASTER` (Webmaster) | `automation.failed` (a scheduled job raised; sent from `core.scheduled_jobs.record_run`, at most once per job per Pacific day) | (alert-only — grants nothing; the alert links to the Automations page (`hub_admin_automations`, #922), which stays `fog_admin_required`) |

Helpers on `Member`: `has_admin_capability(cap)` (the authorization gate) and `sync_admin_capabilities([...], granted_by=…)` (reconcile-to-set, used by the admin member-edit form). Assign/revoke on the **Permissions tab** of the hub Member edit page (`hub.forms.MemberCapabilitiesForm`, admin-only); a full admin can also self-grant from the View As dropdown (`hub.views.view_as_capability_set`).

**Routing mechanics (`core/events/resolvers.py`):** a capability resolver (e.g. `class_approvers`) returns ONLY the capability holders, tagged `capability:<name>`. The capability is the master switch: a `fog_role=admin` member who does not hold it receives nothing (and does not see the row on their settings page — see below); they can self-grant it to opt in. The `guild_leadership_or_{class,events}_approvers` resolvers **compose** (guild present → that guild's leadership ONLY; guild absent → the capability holders) — never a union. Migration `0118` backfills every existing admin with every capability so nobody loses a blanket notification on rollout.

**Settings-page grouping (`core/events/settings_matrix.py`):** every staff/leadership/admin event is collected into one **"Admin / Permissions"** section (rendered first), out of the member-facing categories, and grouped inside it by the permission that brings it (Admin, the eight capability groups by their labels, Guild leadership, Equipment manager; a row shows once, under the first group the viewer holds, `_GROUPS_BY_RECIPIENT`). Each row is shown per-recipient: a capability row only to a holder of that capability, a role row only to that role (`_eligible_for`) — so what the page shows equals what the send path delivers. Plain members never see the section; full admins get a "Manage your admin duties" link to their own capability checkboxes. A forced email never moves a row out of its topic; it only padlocks the Email cell.

**Meeting-item proposal decide is NOT capability-gated:** `hub_meeting_proposal_decide` runs through `can_edit_meeting` (full meeting-workspace edit), so an `EVENTS_APPROVER` who isn't also a guild lead/staff/admin is notified but can't act there — left as-is because widening `can_edit_meeting` would over-grant guild-page/class authority.

## Key QuerySet Methods

- `Member.objects.active()` — status=ACTIVE
- `CommunityEvent.objects.published()` / `scheduled()` / `awaiting_review()` / `past()` / `declined()` — one moderation state each (`past()` is the published one-offs that have ended). Every live surface (the Calendar, Home, the public event page, the Google and Discord pushes, the reminders, the Interested sync) keys on one of them, so a `DRAFT` row (#831: a copy from `CommunityEvent.duplicate`, or a live event taken down by `CommunityEvent.unpublish`) is invisible by construction and shows only on Manage Events, the guild page's Events tab (with a Draft pill) and in the composer, where saving it runs `schedule_or_go_live` (`CommunityEventForm.saves_announce`).
- `Member.objects.instructors()` — active members approved to teach, listed on any class or holding an instructor page, signed in or not; the All Instructors announcement audience (#806)
- `Member.objects.paying()` — member_type=STANDARD
- `Member.objects.with_lease_totals()` — annotates active_lease_count, total_monthly_rent
- `Space.objects.available()` — status=AVAILABLE
- `Space.objects.with_revenue()` — annotates active_lease_rent_total
- `Lease.objects.active(as_of=date)` — start_date≤date and (end_date null or ≥date)
- `EquipmentReservation.objects.holding()` / `confirmed()` (#748) — availability reads (overlap, `ensure_blockable`, free starts, the day timeline, the per member cap, the upcoming lists) use `holding()`, which counts a request awaiting approval, and a priced reservation awaiting payment (#749), because each holds its time; "in use right now" reads (`Equipment.availability_line`, the index card badge, the guild location lights, the Reservations calendar feed) use `confirmed()`, because an undecided request is not the member at the machine.

## Priced equipment (#749)

`Equipment.pricing` is Free, Donation based or Hourly (`hourly_rate_cents`, `donation_minimum_cents`, `donation_suggested_cents`, the orientation donation floor and ceiling). `Equipment.charge_cents_for` is the one price engine (rate times booked minutes over 60, half up to the cent) and `checkout_amount_cents` the one amount check. `membership/equipment.py` copies the paid orientation flow by name: `start_reservation_checkout` holds the time `PENDING_PAYMENT` under the equipment lock and opens Checkout (`kind=equipment_reservation`); `finalize_paid_reservation` is the one money in hand transition (webhook, return page, Pay now, the `release_reservation_payment_holds` sweep); an unpaid hold is deleted, never cancelled. `EquipmentReservation` is a `RefundableSource` (`PaymentRefund.reservation`), and `refund_if_paid` refunds a paid row in full on every decline and cancel, apart from any late fee.

`Equipment.payee` (part 2) is Who gets paid: one of the item's managers, or nobody (the whole payment stays with Past Lives). `EquipmentStaffMembership.delete()` clears the pick when that manager is removed. The books and payouts read it in `billing/` (see `billing/AGENTS.md`). `reserve()` re-runs `checkout_amount_cents` under its lock, so a price or a donation minimum raised after the page read never books free.

## Hourly credit (#787)

`EquipmentCredit` is an append only ledger of minutes per member per hourly item; the balance is the sum (`EquipmentCredit.objects.balance_for`). Managers write GRANTED (the Give Credit card) or UNUSED (Credit Unused Time on a started, confirmed booking, never past its length) and TAKEN_BACK (the whole balance); `reserve()` and `start_reservation_checkout()` read the balance under the Equipment lock, store `EquipmentReservation.credit_minutes` and write a negative SPENT row, charging the rate on the rest (`Equipment.checkout_amount_cents(..., credit_minutes=)`), so a covered booking takes the free road. An unpaid hold's SPENT row cascades with the deleted hold; `cancel()` and `decline()` add a RESTORED row in their transaction and refund the paid part as before. Credit is never money: no refund, reconciliation line or payout reads it. Free and donation items show none.

End Early (part 2): with `Equipment.early_end_credit` on (the Pricing card switch, saved only while Hourly), `EquipmentReservation.end_early` lets the member end their own started, confirmed booking under the Equipment lock: `ends_at` moves back to the last half hour mark (never inside the first half hour) and an ENDED_EARLY row credits the time left rounded up to the half hour, in the member's favour (Felix, 2026-10-12). ENDED_EARLY and UNUSED are `EquipmentCredit.UNUSED_KINDS`: they share the per booking cap, and either one blocks the other and any later cancel or decline. No fee, refund or email.

## Moving an orientation booking (#853)

A leader who may run a booking (`hub._require_can_manage_booking`) moves a requested or confirmed, not yet oriented booking (`OrientationBooking.is_movable`, guarded by `ensure_movable`) from Move to Another Time on the request page or the Bookings tab row menu (`hub_orientation_move`, `hub.forms.OrientationMoveForm`). `orientations.move_orientation` (Move it now) puts it on a fresh one-off slot from `_create_custom_slot` with the old slot's orientor and place, confirms it, stamps `moved_by_guild_at` and emails `orientation_moved` with an ICS of the same UID and a later `SEQUENCE`; the slot it left goes through `_tidy_left_slot` in the same transaction: a posted time (MANUAL, GENERATED) is recapped exactly as a decline does, while a booking's own one-off (`OrientationSlot.OWN_SOURCES`: CUSTOM, the source `_create_custom_slot` now stamps on custom and moved times, and FROM_BLOCK) is deleted when empty or cancelled when it keeps history, so a move never leaves it open. A member cancel or a decline still only recaps it, as before. Every move, proposal and answer re-reads the booking under `locked_booking_queryset()` and decides on that row (`proposal_verdict`: already, stale, passed), so a stale instance can never revive a cancelled booking or accept twice; emails go out after commit. A proposal whose time has passed is no longer open (`has_open_proposal`). `propose_orientation_time` (Ask the member first) only records `proposed_starts_at`, `proposed_at`, `proposed_by` and emails `orientation_time_proposed` with Accept and Keep links: `make_action_token` actions `accept_time` / `keep_time` carrying `proposal_key`, so a replaced or answered proposal's link lands on "no longer open". The member's card buttons post to `hub_orientation_proposal_answer` with the same key; both roads run `answer_proposal`, and both answers bell the request's leaders with no email. A new proposal replaces the open one; decline and cancel clear it. Nothing on any of these roads touches money. `moved_by_guild_at` is the late fee switch: `billing.late_fees._charging_policy` and `late_cancel.booking_cancel_warning` never charge or warn on a moved booking, while its paid amount still refunds in full on cancel.

## Event announcement after the response (#845)

`CommunityEvent.publish()` never sends the member announcement in the request: `owe_announcement` stamps `announcement_owed_since` and hands `finish_announcement` to `core.deferred.defer`, which runs it on a background thread once the save commits. A site-wide send is about 30 seconds of email, bell and push, and gunicorn killed the request at 30 after members were already told, so creators saved again (events 29, 30, 31 on 2026-10-18). `finish_announcement` runs `announce()`, then clears the stamp only if it still holds the value it read; the `finish_event_announcements` job (every 15 minutes, not pausable) finishes any event still stamped after `ANNOUNCEMENT_FINISH_GRACE`. Exactly once rests on the `event:{pk}:published` delivery ledger, which every runner shares. Discord `/create`'s email escalation counts its addresses for the reply (`email_announcement_addresses`) and sends through `send_email_announcement` on its own deferred thread, with no cron backstop. Two known gaps: a hard kill between one member's ledger claim and its send strands that one slot, and an interrupted escalation email is not finished.

**Publishing without announcing.** `publish(announce=False)` and `schedule_or_go_live(announce=False)` still run `push_live` but owe nothing, and set `announcement_held` on an event that was never announced (`was_announced`: the #calendar stamp, its message id, or the `event:{pk}:published` delivery ledger). The composer never writes the hold, only releases it on an untick; `CommunityEventForm.announces` carries the switch to `schedule_or_go_live`. A held event stays quiet through every later publish, the #calendar announcer and the reminders skip it, and only `announce_now` (Manage Events' Announce now, one conditional UPDATE) releases it and owes the announcement. The composer offers the switch to `can_administer_events` only, and the API admits `announce: false` on the same capability (`api.permissions.MayPublishWithoutAnnouncing`); the API's `announce` action on a held event goes through `announce_now`. The weekly #calendar digest leaves a held event out, while the portal's Calendar lists it. Before rolling back to a release without 0241, unpublish held events, or the older announcer announces them. `channel_announced_at` alone cannot say "held": the 15 minute announcer posts every published event whose stamp is blank.

## Removing ex-members from Discord (#898)

`Member.save` queues a `DiscordRemoval` when it moves an existing member from any status to Former and they have Discord linked (`DiscordRemoval.objects.queue_for_former`), so the Airtable pull and an admin's edit both count; Suspended never does. One open row (queued or warned) per Discord id (`uq_discordremoval_open`). The `process_discord_removals` job (weekly, ships off; `membership/discord_removal.py`) DMs anyone whose `warn_on` has come (the day after the Cancellation Date, or after the day the change was seen) and removes the person once `kick_after` (six days after the DM, so the next weekly pass) has passed. Before either step `keep_reason` closes the row if the member is no longer Former (Active, or Paused/Pending from Airtable, which map to Suspended/Invited) or an Active member holds that Discord id. Each row is handled under `select_for_update(skip_locked)` and re-read at its status, so overlapping passes never repeat a step. A closed DM (403) still leads to the removal; a refused removal (403) parks the row as `kick_failed` instead of retrying every pass; a 429, 5xx or network error is retried next pass, and any failure fails the run so the Webmasters hear. With the bot token blank (staging) the pass touches nothing. Every step is an `AutomationAction` (an undelivered DM reads "DM not delivered"); admins cancel an open row from the job's history page through `cancel_locked`, which locks the row and refuses one the job already ran (`RemovalNotOpenError`).

**Coming back.** Discord OAuth asks for `guilds.join` as well, and `DiscordIdentity` carries the token. In `link_and_import`, right after the link is saved, `put_back_if_removed` re-adds an Active member whose account or Discord id has a `kicked` row not yet undone (`awaiting_rejoin`) with `PUT /guilds/{id}/members/{user}` and their token, stamps `rejoined_at`, then pushes a role for every guild membership they hold (app and Discord source), since the kick took them all. It never raises; the link finishes whatever Discord says. A returning member is still linked, so nothing would send them through OAuth: `Member.needs_discord_rejoin` (Active plus such a row) swaps the settings CTA (`hub/partials/_discord_connect_cta.html`) to Rejoin the Discord and reopens the onboarding Discord step under that label, both running the same connect flow.

**The one-time cleanup list.** `manage.py discord_cleanup_list <username>... [--dry-run]` pages the server's members (`list_member_ids_by_username`), matches each username exactly without case, skips one nobody holds, one an Active member has connected, or one already queued (each recorded as Skipped), and queues the rest as `cleanup` rows and DMs them at once with the ticket's fixed text (`DISCORD_CLEANUP_TEXT`, which mentions Felix through `allowed_mentions`). The 15-minute job removes them 3 days later unless they have connected to an active member. The run is recorded under the job's key with the `command` trigger. Usernames are arguments only, never committed; it refuses to run where the bot token is blank.

## Next Meeting and Needs attention (#953)

`Guild.next_meeting_occurrence(sources)` is the soonest of every published event of the guild's own (any type; `is_meeting` only for `GUILD_MEETING`), the cadence or override, the soonest upcoming `Meeting`, and the soonest upcoming Google row (`CalendarEvent`, guild and general feeds, not classes) whose title contains the guild's name, any case. Google rows are read, never linked. A page that lists guilds loads `NextMeetingSources.load(guilds)` once (two queries) and passes it to each call; without it a call loads its own. A timed Meeting row from earlier today no longer counts; an untimed one dated today does. The guild page card and its badge ("Next up" when not `is_meeting`) read the soonest of anything; the Meetings tab, the Meetings page row and "Start the agenda" read `meetings_only=True` (guild meeting events, Meeting rows, the cadence) on the same loaded sources. `has_something_coming_up` adds the TBA flag.

`membership/needs_attention.py` holds `RULES`. The daily `check_needs_attention` job opens a `GuildNeedsAttention` row per guild and broken rule, and emits `guild.needs_attention` (bell only) to the guild's leadership under the period `guild:{pk}:{rule}:{row pk}`, then `...:reminder` to anyone still in the guild's leadership whose first item is 30 days old (reading their earlier item); someone who left has their open item read. One guild that raises is logged and skipped, and the run ends with one `NeedsAttentionCheckError` naming them. A fixed guild closes its row and marks the items read by their link (`Notification.objects.mark_read_for`), so two rules must link to different places. Guild Settings saves run `clear_fixed` at once.
