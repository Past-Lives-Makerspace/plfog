# hq app

The Holiday Bizarre HQ (Track C of the Holiday Bizarre plan). One hub page per event with a hero band, a row of goals and nine door cards, each opening one part of the event. Ships in four parts: part 1 is the feature switch, the HQ page, its cards and the edit pages; part 2 the volunteer shifts behind the `volunteers` card; part 3 (this) the wine wall behind `wine_wall`; C4 adds the seed command and the help article.

## Who

`hq/access.py` holds every rule:

- **The sidebar entry** is the `holiday_bizarre` feature switch (`core/features.py`), seeded Hidden by core migration 0118 so the entry never points at a 404 before the first HQ exists. It is cosmetic, like every switch but My Tab: `/hq/` answers in every state.
- **The open hub** (`EventHub.is_open`, one at a time by a partial unique constraint) is where `/hq/` (`hq:index`) and the sidebar entry land. Its page is public: no login gate, and `hub/base.html` auto-degrades for a signed-out visitor. A closed hub is reachable by admins only (`hub_or_404`), so an HQ can be built before it is shown. With no open hub, `/hq/` sends an admin to `hq:create` and everyone else to the themed 404.
- **A card's audience is the one gate** (`HubCard.is_open_to`): admins and the card's named members always; `public` everyone, signed in or not; `members` an ACTIVE member. A hidden card shows to admins only, with a Hidden chip, and is a 404 at its URL for anyone else (`card_or_404`). A `members` card hit signed out goes through login and back (`card_view`).
- **Rendering reads the effective role** (`request.view_as.is_admin`, `renders_as_admin`), so an admin previewing as a member or a guest sees that viewer's page; `viewer_member` is nobody for a Guest preview. **Action gates read the actual role**: `organizer_required(kind)` admits admins and the card's named members (`HubCard.can_organize`), and the edit pages use `hub.view_as.fog_admin_required`.
- **Guest accounts** (#654) reach `/hq/` on the members surface: `core.middleware.MemberLockoutMiddleware` carves the prefix out before the kiln rule, because the volunteer and wine wall doors are for people with or without an account. What a guest may open is still the card's audience. Two limits, noted and not changed in part 1: `plfog/adapters.py` `pre_login` still refuses a guest signing in on the members host while the kiln switch is off, so the carve-out is reached only by a session opened on the classes host; and a guest on `/hq/` sees the kiln-only sidebar (the guest block of `templates/hub/base.html`).

## Volunteer sign-up (part 2)

`hq/services.py` holds the sheet's logic. The Your Info step is a frozen `VolunteerInfo` (name, email, phone, has_olcc, note) kept in `request.session["hq_volunteer"]`; `person_for(request, member)` is who the buttons act for: the saved step first, then a member's display name, primary email and phone, else nobody (a guest who saved nothing sees Sign Me Up disabled with a hint). Identity on the sheet is the email in Your Info, by design: a visitor who saves an address acts on that address's sign-ups, Remove Me included, and a member who types a different email is matched by that email, not their member row (`member` on a sign-up records who was signed in; it is never the key). `sign_up(shift, person, member=)` locks the shift row and raises `AlreadySignedUp` or `ShiftFull`; `remove(shift, email)` raises `NotSignedUp`. `volunteer_band(hub)` is the strip over the shifts (people by distinct email, spots filled of the total, the most needed shift as the largest open gap, first in page order; None when every shift is full); `roster_rows(hub)` is one row per email with their shifts in page order and their newest details. Guests' sign-ups are counted under `core.abuse_limits` (`hq_signup`, `rate_key(request)`: Cloudflare's address, the rightmost forwarded entry, then `REMOTE_ADDR`); the info form carries a `website` honeypot whose filled value reports success and saves nothing. No notification goes out on a sign-up (follow-up).

Sign Me Up and Remove Me post over htmx and get `partials/_shift_answer.html` back: the refreshed row for the target and the band swapped out of band, with a `trigger_toast`; a plain post (no htmx) lands back on the page with a message. The roster (a table, Copy emails, Download CSV from `hq/exports.py`, registered in `tests/download_links_lint_spec.py`) sits at the foot of the page for organisers (by the effective role, so a preview hides it) and on its own page. Organiser-only pages, the roster and its CSV, answer 403 to anyone else by design, the kiln's `crew_required` shape, rather than the 404 a closed door gives. The volunteers card's edit page carries the shifts formset (`extra=0`, + Add shift, Delete that saves the page); a shift with sign-ups shows the count instead of Delete and the formset refuses such a delete by name.

## Wine wall (part 3)

`hq/services.py` holds the wall's logic. `wine_band(hub)` is the strip over the wall in three queries: bottles (the sum of `qty`), the hub's first `bottles` goal as the meter's target, the value on the wall (`value_cents` times `qty`), the supporters (distinct `giver_name`, case-insensitive) and the bottles by kind; `wineries(hub)` groups the bottles by lowercased winery in two queries (the first spelling logged names the tile) and joins each to its `WineryLogo` by the same key; `donor_rows(hub)` is the organisers' Wine Donors to Thank table, one row per giver with their newest type, email and listing, bottle and value totals and everyone who logged one (a guest as "Guest"). The writes are `log_bottle(hub, fields, member=, logger_name=)`, `delete_bottle(bottle, member=, is_organizer=)` (raises `NotYourBottle` unless the member logged it or is an organiser; the view answers 403) and `set_winery_logo(hub, name, logo)` (replaces the logo under that name, any capitalisation). Who logs is whoever the card is open to; the logger's name is the Your Info name this browser saved, else the member's display name, else blank, and it prefills Credited volunteer. A guest's bottle goes through the same `website` honeypot and the same `hq_signup` rate scope as a shift sign-up. `WineBottleForm` takes the value in dollars and stores cents.

## Views → URLs

| View | URL name | Path |
|------|----------|------|
| `index` | `hq:index` | `/hq/` (302 to the open hub; none → admins to `hq:create`, everyone else 404) |
| `create` | `hq:create` | `/hq/new/` (admin; the hub form, and the nine cards are made with the hub) |
| `hub_page` | `hq:page` | `/hq/<slug>/` (public for an open hub; hero band, goal chips, door cards, footer note; admins see hidden cards, per-card Edit and Edit HQ) |
| `edit` | `hq:edit` | `/hq/<slug>/edit/` (admin; title, eyebrow, event picker over published events, hero via `components/image_field.html`, intro, footer note, goals formset in the Help editor's idiom, Open switch, Save) |
| `hero_delete` | `hq:hero_delete` | `POST /hq/<slug>/hero/delete/` (admin) |
| `card_edit` | `hq:card_edit` | `/hq/<slug>/cards/<pk>/edit/` (admin; words, audience radios, named members as a collapsed pill row that Edit expands into a searchable `.pl-recipient-checklist` of ACTIVE members, Hidden, Move up and Move down) |
| `card_move` | `hq:card_move` | `POST /hq/<slug>/cards/<pk>/move/` with `dir=up` or `down` (admin; lands back on the card's edit page) |
| `volunteers` | `hq:volunteers` | `/hq/<slug>/volunteers/` (`card_view("volunteers")`: the band, Your Info, the shifts by phase, a Wine Wall link when that card is open to the viewer, the roster for organisers) |
| `volunteer_info` | `hq:volunteer_info` | `POST /hq/<slug>/volunteers/info/` (saves the Your Info step to the session) |
| `shift_signup`, `shift_remove` | `hq:shift_signup`, `hq:shift_remove` | `POST /hq/<slug>/volunteers/<shift_pk>/signup/` and `.../remove/` (row and band with a toast over htmx, a message and the page otherwise) |
| `volunteer_roster` | `hq:volunteer_roster` | `/hq/<slug>/volunteers/roster/` (`organizer_required`: admins and the card's named members, 403 for anyone else) |

Templates in `templates/hq/`, styles in `static/css/hq.css` (linked from each page's `extra_head`; the HQ, volunteer and wine pages also load Playfair Display for their display type). The volunteer and wine pages follow Lee's sheets rather than the hub's cards: every rule sits under `.pl-hq-vol` or `.pl-hq-wine`, which share one palette as custom properties, dark by default and light under `[data-theme="light"]`. Specs in `tests/hq/`; the guest carve-out is specced with the lockout middleware in `tests/plfog/member_lockout_spec.py`.
