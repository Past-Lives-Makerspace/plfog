# hq app

The Holiday Bizarre HQ (Track C of the Holiday Bizarre plan). One hub page per event with a hero band, a row of goals and nine door cards, each opening one part of the event. Ships in four parts: part 1 is the feature switch, the HQ page, its cards and the edit pages; part 2 the volunteer shifts behind the `volunteers` card; part 3 the wine wall behind `wine_wall`; part 4 the seed command, the Help Center article and the help keys. Track D turns the six placeholders into live doors, one part each: the Marketing Tracker (D1), the Sponsorship Tracker (D2), Vendor Tables (D3), the Market Check-in (D4), then the event flyer and sharing page (D5).

## Who

`hq/access.py` holds every rule:

- **The sidebar entry** is the `holiday_bizarre` feature switch (`core/features.py`), seeded Hidden by core migration 0118 so the entry never points at a 404 before the first HQ exists. It is cosmetic, like every switch but My Tab: `/hq/` answers in every state.
- **The open hub** (`EventHub.is_open`, one at a time by a partial unique constraint) is where `/hq/` (`hq:index`) and the sidebar entry land. Its page is public: no login gate, and `hub/base.html` auto-degrades for a signed-out visitor. A closed hub is reachable by admins only (`hub_or_404`), so an HQ can be built before it is shown. With no open hub, `/hq/` sends an admin to the newest closed hub, or with no hub at all to `hq:setup` (Load Lee's HQ), and everyone else to the themed 404. There is no blank create form: an HQ is always Lee's content.
- **A card's audience is the one gate** (`HubCard.is_open_to`): admins and the card's named members always; `public` everyone, signed in or not; `members` an ACTIVE member. A hidden card shows to admins only, with a Hidden chip, and is a 404 at its URL for anyone else (`card_or_404`). A `members` card hit signed out goes through login and back (`card_view`).
- **Rendering reads the effective role** (`request.view_as.is_admin`, `renders_as_admin`), so an admin previewing as a member or a guest sees that viewer's page; `viewer_member` is nobody for a Guest preview. **Action gates read the actual role**: `organizer_required(kind)` admits admins and the card's named members (`HubCard.can_organize`), and the edit pages use `hub.view_as.fog_admin_required`.
- **Guest accounts** (#654) reach `/hq/` on the members surface: `core.middleware.MemberLockoutMiddleware` carves the prefix out before the kiln rule, because the volunteer and wine wall doors are for people with or without an account. What a guest may open is still the card's audience. Two limits, noted and not changed in part 1: `plfog/adapters.py` `pre_login` still refuses a guest signing in on the members host while the kiln switch is off, so the carve-out is reached only by a session opened on the classes host; and a guest on `/hq/` sees the kiln-only sidebar (the guest block of `templates/hub/base.html`).

## Volunteer sign-up (part 2)

`hq/services.py` holds the sheet's logic. The Your Info step is a frozen `VolunteerInfo` (name, email, phone, has_olcc, note) kept in `request.session["hq_volunteer"]`; `person_for(request, member)` is who the buttons act for: the saved step first, then a member's display name, primary email and phone, else nobody (a guest who saved nothing sees Sign Me Up disabled with a hint). Identity on the sheet is the email in Your Info, by design: a visitor who saves an address acts on that address's sign-ups, Remove Me included, and a member who types a different email is matched by that email, not their member row (`member` on a sign-up records who was signed in; it is never the key). `sign_up(shift, person, member=)` locks the shift row and raises `AlreadySignedUp` or `ShiftFull`; `remove(shift, email)` raises `NotSignedUp`. `volunteer_band(hub)` is the strip over the shifts (people by distinct email, spots filled of the total, the most needed shift as the largest open gap, first in page order; None when every shift is full); `roster_rows(hub)` is one row per email with their shifts in page order and their newest details. Guests' sign-ups are counted under `core.abuse_limits` (`hq_signup`, `rate_key(request)`: Cloudflare's address, the rightmost forwarded entry, then `REMOTE_ADDR`); the info form carries a `website` honeypot whose filled value reports success and saves nothing. No notification goes out on a sign-up (follow-up).

Sign Me Up and Remove Me post over htmx and get `partials/_shift_answer.html` back: the refreshed row for the target and the band swapped out of band, with a `trigger_toast`; a plain post (no htmx) lands back on the page with a message. The roster (a table, Copy emails, Download CSV from `hq/exports.py`, registered in `tests/download_links_lint_spec.py`) sits at the foot of the page for organisers (by the effective role, so a preview hides it) and on its own page. Organiser-only pages, the roster and its CSV, answer 403 to anyone else by design, the kiln's `crew_required` shape, rather than the 404 a closed door gives. The volunteers card's edit page carries the shifts formset (`extra=0`, + Add shift, Delete that saves the page); a shift with sign-ups shows the count instead of Delete and the formset refuses such a delete by name.

## Wine wall (part 3)

`hq/services.py` holds the wall's logic. `wine_band(hub)` is the strip over the wall in three queries: bottles (the sum of `qty`), the hub's first `bottles` goal as the meter's target, the value on the wall (`value_cents` times `qty`), the supporters (distinct `giver_name`, case-insensitive) and the bottles by kind; `wineries(hub)` groups the bottles by lowercased winery in two queries (the first spelling logged names the tile) and joins each to its `WineryLogo` by the same key; `donor_rows(hub)` is the organisers' Wine Donors to Thank table, one row per giver with their newest type, email and listing, bottle and value totals and everyone who logged one (a guest as "Guest"). The writes are `log_bottle(hub, fields, member=, logger_name=, logger_email=)`, `delete_bottle(bottle, member=, email=, is_organizer=)` (raises `NotYourBottle` unless the member or the email logged it or the caller is an organiser; the view answers 403) and `set_winery_logo(hub, name, logo)` (replaces the logo under that name, any capitalisation). Who logs is whoever the card is open to; identity follows the volunteer sheet: the Your Info name and email this browser saved, else the member's display name and primary email, else blanks. The name prefills Credited volunteer and the email is what a guest's bottles are keyed on, so a guest deletes their own bottle from the same browser (or any browser that saved the same address). A guest's bottle goes through the same `website` honeypot as a sign-up but its own `hq_bottle` rate scope, so a collector logging a case never spends the shift budget. The page renders by the effective role (an admin previewing as a guest sees no donors, logo form or Delete); `bottle_delete`, `winery_logo`, the donors page and the CSV read the actual role. `WineBottleForm` takes the value in dollars and stores cents.

## Seed command and help (part 4)

`hq/holiday_bizarre.py` is Lee's content as data, no ORM: `HUB` (slug `holiday-bizarre-2026`, title, eyebrow, intro, footer note), `CARDS` (the same tuple as `DEFAULT_CARDS`, so the edit page and the seed never drift), `GOALS` (Raised $10,000 typed in, in whole dollars like every money goal; bottles 100, volunteer spots 82, RSVPs 250 and Member tables with no target counted live; a hub seeded before D3 gains Member tables from the seed or Update the doors, below) and `SHIFTS` (her 21 shifts in her words, phase by phase; needs add up to 82; both bartender shifts `requires_olcc`). `manage.py seed_holiday_bizarre [--event <pk>] [--dry-run]` writes them, linked to a PUBLISHED `CommunityEvent` when `--event` names one (an unknown pk or an unpublished event is refused with a `CommandError` listing the published upcoming events); without `--event` the hub has no event until an admin picks it in Edit HQ. The same `seed()` runs behind the Load Lee's HQ button at `/hq/new/`, which is how prod gets it. A command, never a migration (STANDARDS §3 and §10).

It runs twice without duplicates and never touches an admin's edits: everything is written on create only. A hub that exists keeps its title, eyebrow, intro, footer note and open state; only its event moves, when `--event` names a different one, and the output says so (a new hub is open unless another hub already is, which the output names). The doors are `EventHub.seed_default_cards()` (Track D rails, below). Goals are `hq.services.seed_goals(hub)`, which both `seed()` and Update the doors run (the button under its row lock, so a double-submit adds nothing twice): a counted goal (every `HubGoal.Kind` but `manual`) is keyed by its kind, and any of Lee's the hub lacks by kind is added at the end of its goal order and named in the output and in the button's sentence ("added the Member tables goal"); a typed in goal has no key, so the whole set, Raised included, is made only on a hub with no goals. The accepted trade-off: a counted goal an admin deleted comes back on the next run or press. Shifts have no stable key, so the sheet is made only on a hub that holds no shift: a renamed shift never grows a sibling, an edited need survives and a deleted shift stays deleted. `--dry-run` runs the whole seed inside `transaction.atomic()` and `set_rollback(True)`, so its counts are the real ones. Each run prints a line per model and a summary (created, kept). Spec: `tests/hq/seed_spec.py`.

The Help Center article is `holiday-bizarre-hq` in `membership/help_content.py` (Events & Community, right after the Calendar, no screenshots yet: the capture harness has no hub to photograph, so pictures wait on a seeded capture DB). The `hq.*` keys in `core/help_registry.py` (`door-cards`, `goal-tracker`, `volunteer-shifts`, `wine-wall`, `marketing`, `sponsorship`, `vendor-tables`, `market-checkin`, `flyer`, `edit-card`) deep-link into its anchors and are stamped as `data-help-key` on the door grid and goal band (`page.html`), the shift list (`volunteers.html`), the Log a Bottle section (`wine.html`), the Log a Marketing Action section (`partials/_marketing_log.html`), + Add a sponsor (`partials/_sponsor_board.html`), + Add a table request (`partials/_vendor_board.html`), the market report (`partials/_checkin_report.html`), Help Spread the Word (`partials/_flyer_share.html`) and the Who May Open It block (`card_edit.html`); `tests/hq/help_keys_spec.py` holds both ends together.

## Track D rails (D1)

Every Track D door is a live `HubCard.Kind`; its `url` stays blank until its part ships, so the card reads Not shared yet. `hq.models.BUILT_DOORS` is the one set of doors whose page is built (D1: `marketing`; D2: `sponsorship`; D3: `vendor_tables`; D4: `market_checkin`; D5: `flyer`); each later part adds its kind there and nowhere else. `DEFAULT_CARDS` is a tuple of `DoorContent`: the door's eventual kind (`door`), Lee's who-for and title, her current bullets and button (October), and the words it was first seeded with (`old_bullets`, `old_button_text`), and the audience a newly made live door gets (`audience`: admins only, but `flyer`, which Lee opens to members; an adopted placeholder keeps its own). `DoorContent.kind` is the door while it is live (the first three, or built) and `placeholder` otherwise.

`EventHub.seed_default_cards()` is the adoption, and `seed()` and Edit HQ's **Update the doors** (`hq:doors_update`) both run it. A live door is found by kind. A built door the hub still holds as a placeholder with the door's title is converted in place, so its pk, audience, named members, hidden flag and order stay; it is made only when neither the kind nor that placeholder exists (a renamed placeholder is no longer the door's, so the live door is made beside it). Placeholders are made only on a hub with no cards at all, so a deleted one stays deleted; Update the doors runs under a row lock on the hub, so a double-submit adds nothing twice, and a live door made beside a renamed placeholder is named in the message. On every card found, a field whose lines still equal the old default takes Lee's current words; an edited field is kept. Re-running changes nothing. The hub's footer note is never rewritten; a new hub gets Lee's "Can't get in?" line from `HUB`. Update the doors exists because `/hq/new/` only answers before a hub exists, so prod's hub converts with that one click. Spec: `tests/hq/seed_spec.py` (`describe_adopting_a_built_door`) and `tests/hq/edit_spec.py`.

Connections: a figure lives on one door, and every other door, goal and the ledger read it from there. The Marketing Tracker's is `hq.services.marketing_totals(hub)["flyer"]`, "Businesses with flyers" (the ledger, D6, reads it). The Sponsorship Tracker's are `hq.services.sponsor_band(hub).received_cents` and `.committed_cents`, the sponsor money D6's Raised (`EVENT_CASH`) and the ledger read; the band's meter in turn reads the hub's Raised goal target. Vendor Tables' is `hq.services.vendor_band(hub).confirmed`, which the `member_tables` goal (Lee's Member tables, target 0) reads now and the ledger (D6) will. The Market Check-in's are `hq.services.checkin_band(hub)`: total hours, volunteers (members with hours), reports, sales cents, items sold and vendors with sales, the figures the ledger (D6) reads; the volunteer roster reads each member's hours from `hours_by_email(hub)`. The flyer page (D5) reads two: the event's people count (`services.rsvp_count`, the RSVPs goal's source) and `marketing_totals(hub)["flyer"]`, each linked to where it lives.

## Sponsorship Tracker (Track D, part 2)

Lee's tracker behind the `sponsorship` card, on the sign-up sheet's palette with her warn, info and bad colours on top (`.pl-hq-sp`). In her order: the header ("{hub title} · Cash sponsors · Team only"), her guidance line on how to ask, the board (`partials/_sponsor_board.html`, built from `_sponsor_band.html`, `_sponsor_follow_up.html` and one `_sponsor_row.html` per sponsor), and her draft tier table (`hq.holiday_bizarre.SPONSOR_TIERS`, six rows verbatim; the In-kind row's "Wine counts toward the wine wall" links to `hq:wine` when that card is open to the viewer, as the volunteer page's Wine Wall link does).

The board is the band, Follow up, + Add a sponsor and the pipeline. `hq.services.sponsor_band(hub)` is two queries (one aggregate and the goal): received is the amount summed over `received`, committed over `committed`, asked, waiting the ask over `asked` with the open ask count, prospects a count; declined counts nowhere. The meter's target is `sponsor_goal(hub)`: the first goal labelled Raised (any case), else the first typed in money goal, passing over a zero target, else $10,000. The two segments are two stacked `<progress>` bars on one track (received plus committed faded underneath, received solid on top), so the widths need no inline style and both cap at the goal. `follow_ups(hub, today=)` is one query: every sponsor with a date whose ask is open, soonest first, the first eight with "Overdue " or "Today " against the Portland date the view passes (`timezone.localdate()`). The pipeline's filters are `?filter=` links (`SPONSOR_FILTERS`; an unknown value shows everyone, and `donor2025` is history not blank).

The page renders by the effective role. Organisers (`HubCard.can_organize`) get the contact details, Follow up's rows, the add form, each row as a `<details>` that opens to its edit form, the 2025 box, the contact log and its add form, Delete sponsor through the confirm modal, the 2025 donors filter and Download CSV. Everyone else the card is open to gets the band, the follow-up count, and each sponsor's name, tier and status (a `donor2025` filter falls back to everyone for them). Every POST and the CSV are `organizer_required` (the actual role; 403 for anyone else). Each form posts over htmx with the filter in a hidden field. + Add a sponsor gets the whole board back, so the new row lands in its place in the pipeline. A row's Save and Add to log target that row only (`hx-target="closest [data-hq-sp-row]"`, outerHTML) and get `partials/_sponsor_row_answer.html`: the row, open (a refused one with its errors), with the band and Follow up swapped out of band by id, so unsaved typing in other open rows and in the add form survives. The row comes back even when it no longer matches the filter (a status change under `?filter=asked`), and a refused plain post renders the page with that row kept in the list (`filtered_sponsors(..., keep_pk=)`). The toasts are "Sponsor added.", "Saved.", "Added to the log."; a successful plain post redirects to the page with the filter and a message, and Delete is a plain post. The money fields take whole dollars up to `SPONSOR_MAX_DOLLARS` ($1,000,000): the cents columns are Postgres integers, and an unbounded figure would overflow as a 500 rather than a form error. One Save per row rather than Lee's save on every change: the repo's autosave idiom (`guild_autosave.js`) is a whole-page formset, not a list of independent rows. ## Vendor Tables (Track D, part 3)

Only + Add a table request redraws the board, and keeps the new row in view under a filter it does not match (`keep_pk`). Save capacity swaps its own form with the band out of band (`capacity` 1 to `VENDOR_CAPACITY_MAX`, blank clears it).
