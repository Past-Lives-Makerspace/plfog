# hq app

The Holiday Bizarre HQ (Track C of the Holiday Bizarre plan). One hub page per event with a hero band, a row of goals and nine door cards, each opening one part of the event. Ships in four parts: part 1 is the feature switch, the HQ page, its cards and the edit pages; part 2 the volunteer shifts behind the `volunteers` card; part 3 the wine wall behind `wine_wall`; part 4 the seed command. Track D turns the six placeholders into live doors, one part each: the Marketing Tracker (D1), the Sponsorship Tracker (D2), Vendor Tables (D3), the Market Check-in (D4), the event flyer and sharing page (D5), then the PL Ledger in three parts, the budget first (D6a), then the money in (D6b: drink sales, the wine wall's tickets and the event cash Raised counts), then the team and tally (D6c: the running tally and goals read from every door, attendance with a door clicker, the launch checklist, staff time and guild demos).

## Who

`hq/access.py` and `HubCard` hold every rule (HQ card sharing, Felix 2026-10-22; spec `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-hq-card-sharing.md`):

- **Each card has an audience and two people lists.** The audience is Admins only, Members (an ACTIVE member) or Everyone (signed in or not). **Can open** people see the card and use it as a member would: sign up for shifts, log a bottle, file a check-in, read the page. The **Team** gets everything Can open gives plus the card's organiser work: add, edit and delete its rows, mark donors thanked, see rosters, donors and reports, download the CSVs. The Team is the old named members, renamed in place by hq migration 0013 (the field keeps its table, `hq_hubcard_named_members`, so nobody lost access and the release serving during the deploy reads the same rows; `tests/hq/team_migration_spec.py`).
- **`HubCard.is_open_to` is the one gate**: admins and the Team always; on any door but a Team-only one, the Can open people, `public` everyone and `members` an ACTIVE member. `HubCardQuerySet.open_to(member)` is the same rule as one query (hidden cards left out). `can_organize` is admins and the Team. A hidden card shows to admins only, with a Hidden chip, and is a 404 at its URL for anyone else (`card_or_404`). A `members` card hit signed out goes through login and back (`card_view`).
- **Team-only doors** (`TEAM_ONLY_KINDS`: `vendor_tables`, `ledger`) are pages only the Team may use (`organizer_page`), so a wider audience or a Can open list would put a door on someone's HQ that answers 403. Their share panel offers only Admins only and the Team; `HubCard.clean` refuses any other audience and `CardSharingForm` any Can open list; and `is_open_to` ignores both whatever the database says. Every other kind's page is a `card_view` a member can use: Volunteer Sign-up, the wine wall, the Marketing and Sponsorship Trackers, the Market Check-in (its reports and hours pages are `organizer_page`, linked for the Team only) and the flyer page; the RSVP door is the event page; a placeholder opens nothing.
- **Settings and uploads are admins only, whatever the lists say** (`admin_required(kind)`, the actual role; 403 for anyone else, 404 for a hub without the door): the flyer files and links (`flyer_upload`, `flyer_remove`, `flyer_links`), winery logos (`winery_logo`), the ledger's approval (`ledger_approval`), drink and ticket prices (`ledger_bar_prices`, `ledger_ticket_prices`), budget lines (`ledger_line_add`, `ledger_line_edit`, `ledger_line_delete`) the vendor floor's capacity (`vendor_floor`) and the vendor sheet's link (`vendor_sheet`), beside card edit, card move, card share, hub edit, hero and Update the doors (`fog_admin_required`). Each such control renders by the effective role (`request.view_as.is_admin` in the template, or the view's `is_admin`), so an admin previewing as a member sees that member's page. The Team still logs ledger entries and receipts against the budget lines, the bar, tickets sold, the door, the checklist, staff time and guild demos. Every other `organizer_required` action is the Team's.
- **Rendering reads the effective role** (`request.view_as.is_admin`, `renders_as_admin`), so an admin previewing as a member or a guest sees that viewer's page; `viewer_member` is nobody for a Guest preview. **Action gates read the actual role** (`organizer_required`, `admin_required`, `check_in_required`).
- **The sidebar entry** has no feature switch (the `holiday_bizarre` switch was retired by core migration 0119). `hub.context_processors._hq_nav` shows it to an admin, by the effective role, whenever any HQ exists, and to anyone else once a card on the open HQ is opened to them (`HubCardQuerySet.open_to`), in one lazy query on every hub page. It is one partial (`hub/partials/_sidebar_hq_link.html`) in all three blocks of `hub/base.html`, the guest accounts' kiln menu included. Admin Tools keeps its Holiday Bizarre HQ card. Spec: `tests/hq/sidebar_spec.py`.
- **The open hub** (`EventHub.is_open`, one at a time by a partial unique constraint) is where `/hq/` (`hq:index`) and the sidebar entry land. Its page shows a non-admin only the doors they may open; with none, signed in or out, it is the themed 404 (so a signed-out visitor sees the Everyone doors, or a 404). A closed hub is reachable by admins only (`hub_or_404`), so an HQ can be built before it is shown. With no open hub, `/hq/` sends an admin to the newest closed hub, or with no hub at all to `hq:setup` (Load Lee's HQ), and everyone else to the themed 404. There is no blank create form: an HQ is always Lee's content.
- **The share panel** (`hq:card_share`, `CardSharingForm`, `partials/_share_form.html`): admins (by the effective role) see under each card who can open it (`partials/_share_chip.html`: "Admins only", "Members" or "Everyone", plus "· N people" when the lists name anyone) and a Share link that loads the panel into a `components/modal.html` over htmx (with no script it is the panel's own page, `hq/card_share.html`). The audience is three plain choices, each with one line (`components/radio_group.html`'s `choice_hints`); Can open and Team are the `hqPeoplePicker` pickers (`static/js/hq_people_picker.js`, `partials/_people_picker.html`, the `inquiryMemberPicker` pattern keeping several people): chips with name and email, each removable, over a search box whose list shows while typing. They offer ACTIVE members plus anyone already on a list (shown even when no longer active, so they can be removed); someone on both lists is kept on the Team alone. Saving is one POST, admin only by the actual role: over htmx it answers with the panel, the chip out of band, a toast and `hq-share-saved` (which closes the modal); a plain post lands on the HQ page. Card edit carries the same fields and pickers (`HubCardForm` extends `CardSharingForm`), so there is one way to share. Spec: `tests/hq/sharing_spec.py`, and `tests/e2e/hq_card_sharing_spec.py` drives it in a browser.
- **Guest accounts** (#654) reach `/hq/` on the members surface: `core.middleware.MemberLockoutMiddleware` carves the prefix out before the kiln rule, because the volunteer and wine wall doors are for people with or without an account. What a guest may open is still the card's audience and lists. One limit, noted and not changed: `plfog/adapters.py` `pre_login` still refuses a guest signing in on the members host while the kiln switch is off, so the carve-out is reached only by a session opened on the classes host.

## Volunteer sign-up (part 2)

`hq/services.py` holds the sheet's logic. The Your Info step is a frozen `VolunteerInfo` (name, email, phone, has_olcc, note) kept in `request.session["hq_volunteer"]`; `person_for(request, member)` is who the buttons act for: the saved step first, then a member's display name, primary email and phone, else nobody (a guest who saved nothing sees Sign Me Up disabled with a hint). Identity on the sheet is the email in Your Info, by design: a visitor who saves an address acts on that address's sign-ups, Remove Me included, and a member who types a different email is matched by that email, not their member row (`member` on a sign-up records who was signed in; it is never the key). `sign_up(shift, person, member=)` locks the shift row and raises `AlreadySignedUp` or `ShiftFull`; `remove(shift, email)` raises `NotSignedUp`. `volunteer_band(hub)` is the strip over the shifts (people by distinct email, spots filled of the total, the most needed shift as the largest open gap, first in page order; None when every shift is full); `roster_rows(hub)` is one row per email with their shifts in page order and their newest details. Guests' sign-ups are counted under `core.abuse_limits` (`hq_signup`, `rate_key(request)`: Cloudflare's address, the rightmost forwarded entry, then `REMOTE_ADDR`); the info form carries a `website` honeypot whose filled value reports success and saves nothing. No notification goes out on a sign-up (follow-up).

Sign Me Up and Remove Me post over htmx and get `partials/_shift_answer.html` back: the refreshed row for the target and the band swapped out of band, with a `trigger_toast`; a plain post (no htmx) lands back on the page with a message. The roster (a table, Copy emails, Download CSV from `hq/exports.py`, registered in `tests/download_links_lint_spec.py`) sits at the foot of the page for organisers (by the effective role, so a preview hides it) and on its own page. Organiser-only pages, the roster and its CSV, answer 403 to anyone else by design, the kiln's `crew_required` shape, rather than the 404 a closed door gives. The volunteers card's edit page carries the shifts formset (`extra=0`, + Add shift, Delete that saves the page); a shift with sign-ups shows the count instead of Delete and the formset refuses such a delete by name.
