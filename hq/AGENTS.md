# hq app

The Holiday Bizarre HQ (Track C of the Holiday Bizarre plan). One hub page per event with a hero band, a row of goals and four door cards, each opening one part of the event: RSVP (the event page), Volunteer Sign-up (Track C part 2), Vendor Tables (Track D part 3) and the PL Ledger (D6a, the budget; D6b, drink sales and the event cash Raised counts; D6c, the running tally and goals, attendance with a door clicker, the launch checklist, staff time and guild demos). Part 4 of Track C is the seed command. #1307 opened the HQ to guild officers (part 1) and removed the wine wall, the Marketing and Sponsorship Trackers, the Market Check-in and the event flyer and sharing page, with their models and tables (part 2, hq migration 0019).

## Who

`hq/access.py` holds the one rule (#1307; guild officer per ADR 0004):

- **Admins and every ACTIVE guild officer run the whole HQ**, nobody else. An officer is an ACTIVE member who leads or staffs any guild (`Member.staffed_guilds`); there is no role or capability for it. `can_run_hq(member, *, is_admin)` is the rule and `hq_gate(kind=None)` the one decorator on every HQ view: the HQ page, every card's page, rows, CSVs, settings and uploads (budget lines, prices, the approval, the vendor floor and its Discord channel), Edit HQ, hero, Update the doors, card edit, card move and Load Lee's HQ. An officer does everything an admin does. It is read per request, so someone who stops leading or staffing a guild loses the HQ on their next request.
- **Rendering reads the effective role, actions the actual one.** A safe method (GET, HEAD) asks `runs_hq(request)` (`request.view_as`), so an admin previewing as a member sees that member's answer and as a Guest the 404; any other method asks by the actual role, so a preview never grants or takes it away.
- **Everyone else**: signed out goes through login and back (`redirect_to_login`); a signed-in member who leads or staffs no guild gets the themed 404, never a 403, so the HQ never reveals itself. A guest account is never ACTIVE, and `core.middleware.MemberLockoutMiddleware` keeps it off `/hq/` like any other members page.
- **Hidden** is a staging marker, not a gate: everyone who runs the HQ sees a hidden card with its Hidden chip, opens it and can unhide it on card edit (an officer does everything an admin does). `card_or_404` 404s only a kind the hub lacks, and links from other doors (`_door_url`, `_door_urls`) ignore the flag.
- **Cards have no audience, Can open list, Team or Share panel** (dropped by hq migration 0018, with the `hq_hubcard_can_open` and `hq_hubcard_named_members` tables). Where the Team used to supply names (the Who, Owner, Who's asking and Contacted by suggestions, and the person a ledger staff time row links to) it is `officers()`, every ACTIVE guild officer. Vendor Tables waits on "the vendor team".
- **The sidebar entry** (`hub.context_processors._hq_nav`) shows whenever any HQ exists to whoever runs it, by the effective role, in at most two lazy queries. It is one partial (`hub/partials/_sidebar_hq_link.html`) in every block of `hub/base.html`. Spec: `tests/hq/sidebar_spec.py`.
- **The open hub** (`EventHub.is_open`, one at a time) is where `/hq/` lands; with none open, the newest closed hub, and with no hub at all Load Lee's HQ (`hq:setup`). Whoever runs the HQ reaches a closed hub too, so one can be built before it opens.

## Volunteer sign-up (part 2)

`hq/services.py` holds the sheet's logic. The Your Info step is a frozen `VolunteerInfo` (name, email, phone, has_olcc, note) kept in `request.session["hq_volunteer"]`; `person_for(request, member)` is who the buttons act for: the saved step first, then a member's display name, primary email and phone, else nobody (a guest who saved nothing sees Sign Me Up disabled with a hint). Identity on the sheet is the email in Your Info, by design: a visitor who saves an address acts on that address's sign-ups, Remove Me included, and a member who types a different email is matched by that email, not their member row (`member` on a sign-up records who was signed in; it is never the key). `sign_up(shift, person, member=)` locks the shift row and raises `AlreadySignedUp` or `ShiftFull`; `remove(shift, email)` raises `NotSignedUp`. `volunteer_band(hub)` is the strip over the shifts (people by distinct email, spots filled of the total, the most needed shift as the largest open gap, first in page order; None when every shift is full); `roster_rows(hub)` is one row per email with their shifts in page order and their newest details. Guests' sign-ups are counted under `core.abuse_limits` (`hq_signup`, `rate_key(request)`: Cloudflare's address, the rightmost forwarded entry, then `REMOTE_ADDR`); the info form carries a `website` honeypot whose filled value reports success and saves nothing. No notification goes out on a sign-up (follow-up).

Sign Me Up and Remove Me post over htmx and get `partials/_shift_answer.html` back: the refreshed row for the target and the band swapped out of band, with a `trigger_toast`; a plain post (no htmx) lands back on the page with a message. The roster (a table, Copy emails, Download CSV from `hq/exports.py`, registered in `tests/download_links_lint_spec.py`) sits at the foot of the page for organisers (by the effective role, so a preview hides it) and on its own page. Organiser-only pages, the roster and its CSV, answer 403 to anyone else by design, the kiln's `crew_required` shape, rather than the 404 a closed door gives. The volunteers card's edit page carries the shifts formset (`extra=0`, + Add shift, Delete that saves the page); a shift with sign-ups shows the count instead of Delete and the formset refuses such a delete by name.

## Seed command (part 4)

`hq/holiday_bizarre.py` is Lee's content as data, no ORM: `HUB` (slug `holiday-bizarre-2026`, title, eyebrow, intro, footer note), `CARDS` (the same tuple as `DEFAULT_CARDS`, so the edit page and the seed never drift), `GOALS` (all five counted live: Raised $10,000, the event cash since D6b (the bar's sales since #1307), in whole dollars like every money goal; volunteer spots 82, RSVPs 250, and Member tables and Guilds taking part (D6c) with no target; a hub seeded before D3 or D6c gains the missing ones from the seed or Update the doors, below) and `SHIFTS` (her 21 shifts in her words, phase by phase; needs add up to 82; both bartender shifts `requires_olcc`). `manage.py seed_holiday_bizarre [--event <pk>] [--dry-run]` writes them, linked to a PUBLISHED `CommunityEvent` when `--event` names one (an unknown pk or an unpublished event is refused with a `CommandError` listing the published upcoming events); without `--event` the hub has no event until an admin picks it in Edit HQ. The same `seed()` runs behind the Load Lee's HQ button at `/hq/new/`, which is how prod gets it. A command, never a migration (STANDARDS §3 and §10).

It runs twice without duplicates and never touches an admin's edits: everything is written on create only. A hub that exists keeps its title, eyebrow, intro, footer note and open state; only its event moves, when `--event` names a different one, and the output says so (a new hub is open unless another hub already is, which the output names). The doors are `EventHub.seed_default_cards()` (Track D rails, below). Goals are `hq.services.seed_goals(hub)`, which both `seed()` and Update the doors run (the button under its row lock, so a double-submit adds nothing twice): a counted goal (every `HubGoal.Kind` but `manual`) is keyed by its kind, and any of Lee's the hub lacks by kind is added at the end of its goal order and named in the output and in the button's sentence ("added the Member tables goal"); a typed in goal has no key, so one is made only on a hub with no goals (Lee's set has none since D6b). Before that, and only while the hub has no `event_cash` goal, `_adopt_raised` turns one typed in goal into `event_cash` in place, keeping its pk, label, target and order and marking it money, so prod's typed Raised is never doubled by the counted one: the manual goal labelled Raised (any case, trimmed; the first in band order) if there is one, else the hub's one manual money goal whatever its label ("Cash raised"), since it can only be the money raised. With two or more manual money goals and none labelled Raised it adopts nothing, and the top-up adds the counted Raised beside them. The output and the button's sentence say "Raised now counts itself" whatever the adopted label (`GoalSeed.adopted` holds the label, `CardSeed.goals_adopted` carries it). The accepted trade-off: a counted goal an admin deleted, Raised included, comes back on the next run or press. Shifts have no stable key, so the sheet is made only on a hub that holds no shift: a renamed shift never grows a sibling, an edited need survives and a deleted shift stays deleted. `--dry-run` runs the whole seed inside `transaction.atomic()` and `set_rollback(True)`, so its counts are the real ones. Each run prints a line per model and a summary (created, kept). Spec: `tests/hq/seed_spec.py`.

The HQ has no Help Center guide and no `hq.*` help keys (Felix, 2026-10-20). The guide `holiday-bizarre-hq` shipped once, so its slug stays in `LEGACY_SLUG_MAP` (`membership/help_content.py`) and `seed_help_center` unpublishes the row; do not add it back to `ARTICLES`.

## Track D rails (D1)

Every Track D door is a live `HubCard.Kind`; its `url` stays blank until its part ships, so the card reads Not shared yet. `hq.models.BUILT_DOORS` is the set of Track D doors still built: `vendor_tables` (D3) and `ledger` (D6a). #1307 removed `marketing` (D1), `sponsorship` (D2), `market_checkin` (D4) and `flyer` (D5), and the first three's `wine_wall`; `DEFAULT_CARDS` no longer names them, so the seed and Update the doors never bring them back. With every door built, the seed makes no placeholder; the placeholder rail stays for a door not yet built and is specced by taking the ledger out of `BUILT_DOORS` (`seed_spec`). `DEFAULT_CARDS` is a tuple of `DoorContent`: the door's eventual kind (`door`), Lee's who-for and title, her current bullets and button (October), and the words it was first seeded with (`old_bullets`, `old_button_text`). `DoorContent.kind` is the door while it is live (the first three, or built) and `placeholder` otherwise.

`EventHub.seed_default_cards()` is the adoption, and `seed()` and Edit HQ's **Update the doors** (`hq:doors_update`) both run it. A live door is found by kind. A built door the hub still holds as a placeholder with the door's title is converted in place, so its pk, hidden flag and order stay; it is made only when neither the kind nor that placeholder exists (a renamed placeholder is no longer the door's, so the live door is made beside it). Placeholders are made only on a hub with no cards at all, so a deleted one stays deleted; Update the doors runs under a row lock on the hub, so a double-submit adds nothing twice, and a live door made beside a renamed placeholder is named in the message. On every card found, a field whose lines still equal the old default takes Lee's current words; an edited field is kept. Re-running changes nothing. The hub's footer note is never rewritten; a new hub gets Lee's "Can't get in?" line from `HUB`. Update the doors exists because `/hq/new/` only answers before a hub exists, so prod's hub converts with that one click. Spec: `tests/hq/seed_spec.py` (`describe_adopting_a_built_door`) and `tests/hq/edit_spec.py`.

Connections: a figure lives on one door, and every other door, the hub's goals and the ledger read it live from there and link back to it; nothing is typed twice. The whole map is **Every door connected**, below.

## Vendor Tables (Track D, part 3)

The page builds `services.door_figures(hub, band, settings)` once and hands its bar to the section, so it and the board paragraph read one set of figures. #1307 removed the Wine Wall section and its tickets with the wine wall door.

**Drink Sales.** The tiles (`_ledger_bar_tiles.html`, `services.BarTotals` from `bar_totals(hub, settings, cost_cents=band.bar_cost_cents)`, one query grouped by drink and way to pay): Drinks sold with Lee's "10 IPA · 5 Pilsner" line, Sales with "2025: about $500" (`last_year_bar_cents`; "Ahead of" or "Behind" 2025 once there are sales), Bar cost (D6a's Drinks lines only, read, never typed), Bar profit (sales less cost, Lee's minus sign and colours), and a gold meter of sales toward the cost under Lee's break-even line: "Break-even: about **N** more beers at $IPA" (`beers_to_break_even`, the shortfall over the IPA price rounded up, her `Math.ceil(need / P.ipa)`), "Break-even reached", "Break-even shows once costs are logged", or the dollars to go when the IPA is free. Quick add during the event is Paid by (Lee's four, `components/radio_group.html` as chips) and a large +1 button per priced drink (`_ledger_bar_buttons.html`: the drink, how many have sold, its price; `aria-label="Add one IPA at $6"`), each a submit button of one form posting `quick-drink` with `quick-pay` (`BarQuickForm`). A tap is one `BarSale` at the hub's price (`log_quick_sale`), source quick, by the actual member. It posts with `hx-swap="none"` and gets `_ledger_bar_answer.html`: the tiles, the buttons and the table, then (D6c) the tally, the goals, the board panel's figures and Staff time's tiles (Every door connected, above), each out of band in a `<template>`, with Lee's toast ("Added 1 IPA (Card)."); Paid by is never swapped, so it keeps its choice for the next tap. While a tap is in flight every +1 is disabled (`hx-disabled-elt="[data-hq-lg-quick]"` on the form; Undo last's form has `find button`), so a double tap is one sale; the answer's buttons arrive enabled and htmx re-enables Undo last (checked in a browser with the request held). **Undo last** (`undo_quick_sale`) deletes the newest quick sale the signed in organiser tapped, under a row lock, never anyone else's and never a batch ("Took back 1 IPA (Card).", or "Nothing of yours to undo." as an info toast). A plain post of either lands on `#hq-lg-bar` with a message. Set drink prices (`DrinkPricesForm`, `_ledger_bar_prices.html`) takes the four prices (up to `PRICE_MAX_DOLLARS`, $1,000) and last year's bar sales, swaps itself and sends the buttons, the tiles and the goals out of band. Log a batch (`BarBatchForm`, `_ledger_bar_batch.html`): Drink (Mixed included), How many (0 to 10,000), Total collected, Paid by, Note; a blank total is the price times how many, Mixed needs a total ("For mixed sales, enter the total collected."), neither drinks nor money is refused ("Enter how many, or a total collected."), and a price times count over $1,000,000 is refused rather than overflowing; a saved batch swaps the form back open keeping the drink and Paid by, with the tiles and the table out of band and every figure the tap sends ("Sales added."). The table (`_ledger_bar_table.html`) is newest first, each sale with a Delete through the confirm modal (`ledger_bar_delete`, a plain post; labelled with the sale and its time), and Download drink sales (CSV) once there is a sale: `hq.exports.stream_bar_sales_csv`, Lee's columns (Time, Drink, Qty, Amount, Paid by, Note, Logged as) oldest first, then a blank row and her totals row (the drinks, the sales, each way to pay including the $5 RSVP credit her code left out, Bar cost, Profit). A budget line's Save sends the tiles, the goals and the board panel's figures out of band too, since moving a line into or out of Drinks moves the bar's cost.


**Stale saves.** Set drink prices and the approval carry the figures they were rendered with as hidden `seen_*` fields (`forms._SeenFigures`: `FIGURES` as whole numbers, `TEXT_FIGURES` as text); `services.save_ledger_figures` compares them under a row lock and writes nothing when they differ (a missing figure never matches), and the form comes back with what the database holds and `SETTINGS_STALE_SAVE` (the approval's own words for it). Before the first save the comparison is against Lee's defaults, and the row is made only when the save goes ahead, so a refusal writes nothing at all; a first save racing another is held to the row the other made. Each form writes only its own fields, so one form's save never makes another stale.

**Raised counts itself (Felix, 2026-10-20; #1307).** `HubGoal.Kind.EVENT_CASH` ("Event cash") is `event_cash(hub)`: the bar's sales since the sponsors and the wine wall tickets went with their doors, in whole dollars rounded down, two queries however many goals share it (`goal_rows` stays a fixed number). Its target is `cash_goal(hub)`: the hub's event cash goal, else a goal labelled Raised, else a typed in money goal, else $10,000. `GOALS`'s Raised is that kind, and the goal top-up adopts a typed in Raised in place (Seed command and help, above). The ledger page and a tap's answer are each pinned to a fixed number of queries as sales grow. Spec: `tests/hq/ledger_money_spec.py`.
