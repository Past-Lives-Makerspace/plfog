# kiln app

Kiln tickets for the Ceramics Guild (#691). A maker files one ticket per piece (or set of identical pieces) with photos and the guild's questions; the crew loads, unloads and tells them it is ready. Ships in three parts: part 1 is tickets for makers and the crew's clay and glaze lists; part 2 adds loading, crew flags and replies; part 3 adds unloading, pickup notices and the kiln log.

## Who

`kiln/access.py` holds every rule:

- The kiln guild is found by `settings.KILN_GUILD_SLUG` (`ceramics-guild`, guild 8 in production). No pk is hard coded.
- **Crew** is that guild's `guild_lead` plus its `GuildStaffMembership` rows (`is_crew`). Crew notifications (`Recipients.KILN_CREW`), the `staff` maker type and the crew row on the settings page follow `is_crew` alone.
- **Site admins** (`is_fog_admin`) see every crew screen too, switch or no switch, through `can_run_kiln` (what `crew_required`, `can_reach_kiln` and the crew branches of the views read), but they are not crew: no crew notifications, no staff maker type. No new role.
- **Makers** are any member the portal admits plus any **guest** account (#654). A guest reaches `/kiln/` and nothing else on the members surface: `core.middleware.MemberLockoutMiddleware` sends every other members page to `kiln:mine`, and `AdminRedirectAccountAdapter.pre_login` lets a guest sign in. Former and suspended members stay locked out. A guest's sidebar is Kiln Tickets and Sign out (`KilnNav.guest_only` in `templates/hub/base.html`), and `/kiln/` is a guest's home.
- **Launch switch:** `SiteConfiguration.kiln_tickets_open` (Site Settings, General tab), off by default. Off: only the crew and admins reach `/kiln/` (`kiln_open_required` answers everyone else 404) and see the guild page's Kiln Tickets tab, and guests are locked out exactly as before #691 (the middleware and `pre_login` read it through `guest_may_use_kiln`). On: everything below.
- **Where it lives:** Kiln Tickets is a tab on the Ceramics Guild page (`?tab=kiln`, `kiln.access.kiln_home_url`), not a sidebar entry; it concerns that guild alone. The tab shows on the kiln guild's page only, on the members surface only, to a signed in maker the launch gate admits (`kiln.services.guild_kiln_home`, which returns None before any query on every other guild). It renders `kiln/partials/_guild_tab.html`: the maker's home (`_home.html`, the same rows as `/kiln/`) and, for the crew and admins, links to Load the Kiln and Unload with their counts, the Kiln Log and the lists. `/kiln/` (`kiln:mine`) redirects everyone but a guest to the tab (keeping `?older=1`), so notification links and bookmarks that say `/kiln/` still land. Every other kiln page keeps its URL; its breadcrumb (`kiln/partials/_crumb.html`) and the crew tab row's My Tickets go to the tab (`KilnNav.home_url`, a guest's `/kiln/`), and the sidebar lights the Ceramics Guild entry under Guilds on any `/kiln/` page.

## Models

| Model | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| `ClayOption`, `GlazeOption` | The guild's lists. Archived (`archived_at`, `archived_by`), never deleted; a partial unique constraint keeps active names unique. Seeded by migration 0002. |
| `KilnTicket` | `status` holds the whole lifecycle (draft, submitted "In the queue", loaded "In the kiln", fired "Ready for pickup"); loading moves submitted to loaded, unloading moves loaded to fired (with `fired_at`) or back to submitted. Any status can be deleted (`delete_with_photos`). `firing` is set when it is loaded and cleared when the piece goes back to the queue. `objects.newest_fired_first()` is a maker's home order (history by `fired_at`; `kiln.services.kiln_home` builds both homes in a fixed number of queries). `objects.waiting()` is the crew's queue, longest wait first; `objects.visible_to(member, crew=...)` is who may open it. Name and contact are read from `maker`; `maker_type` is a snapshot taken on every save. `missing_for_submit` is the one list of what Submit refuses for; `automatic_flag_kinds` / `sync_automatic_flags` are the flag rules. |
| `KilnTicketPhoto` | `image` (gallery size) and `tile` (480px) from one upload (`kiln.models.store_photo`, the one resize path for every kiln photo); a partial unique constraint allows one cover, and `add_photo` / `remove_photo` / `set_cover` keep exactly one while photos exist. |
| `KilnFlag` | Automatic flags have no `added_by`; the maker sees each as a kind note (`MAKER_NOTES`). Crew flags are `MANUAL` with a `note` and `added_by` (`KilnTicket.add_flag`), and the maker never sees them or their note (`maker_flags`; `crew_flags` is the crew's list). `clear(by)` keeps the first clear. One automatic flag per kind per ticket. |
| `KilnFiring` | One load: `firing_type`, `number` (one sequence across bisque and glaze, "Glaze firing 88", unique; `start` takes the next free number if two crews confirm at once), `loaded_by`, `loaded_at`, then `unloaded_by`, `unloaded_at` (set once). `objects.in_kiln()` is what Unload lists. `exceptions` are the unload notes on it; `exceptions_label` is the log's "1 exception: cracked". |
| `KilnReply` | The thread on a ticket: `author` (the maker, or crew), `body`, `created_at`. `from_crew` is any author but the maker. A note the crew writes at an unload is a reply too, with `firing`, `what_happened` (cracked, glaze ran, stuck to the shelf, something else) and `outcome` (fired, or back to the queue); a check constraint keeps the three together. It is the exception record: the timeline's "Back to the queue" (`KilnTicket.returned_to_queue`), the thread's tag and the log's note all read it. A crew message or unload note may carry one photo (#1026): `image` and `tile` (nullable, so old code can still insert a reply mid deploy), written by `attach_photo` through `store_photo`; `has_photo`, `photo_files`. |

## Rules worth knowing

- Submit is refused only for: a photo, a firing type, a clay (an Other clay needs its name), the Cone 6 confirmation that applies, and the follow up text of a ticked glaze kind. Every other answer only flags.
- Saving drops the answers of the branch the maker left (`drop_inapplicable_answers`), so a glaze answer never flags a bisque piece.
- A ticket is editable until loaded. Any save of a ticket already in the queue is checked like a Submit and re-runs the automatic flags; a flag the answers still raise keeps its cleared state.
- "Make another like this" is `kiln:new?from=<pk>`: every answer but the photos and the Cone 6 confirmations (`copy_initial`). Nothing is written until the maker saves.
- The ticket form is one form with several submit buttons (`action` = draft, submit, `cover:<pk>`, `remove:<pk>`); the photo buttons save the whole page first. It is not boosted (file uploads). `static/js/kiln_ticket_form.js` previews picks and keeps earlier picks when the maker adds more.
- **Loading** (`kiln.services.load_kiln`): one transaction locks every ticked ticket (`select_for_update`) before reading it, loads only those still in the queue and of the firing's type, and makes the firing only if something goes in. A piece another crew member just loaded, or the maker changed, is skipped and counted in the toast. Makers are not notified on load. `save_ticket` takes the same row lock and refuses a maker's edit that lands after the load (`TicketNotEditable`), so an edit can never write the queue status back over it.
- **Photos on crew messages and unload notes** (#1026): optional, never required, one per message. The crew's box on another maker's ticket (`kiln.forms.ReplyForm(takes_photo=True)`, `kiln.views._takes_photo`) and an unticked row on Unload (`photo-<pk>`) take one through `kiln/partials/_photo_pick.html` (take or choose, like the ticket form's camera input; `static/js/kiln_photo_pick.js` shows the pick and clears the other input). Both forms are multipart and not boosted. Uploads are checked like ticket photos (`kiln.forms.PHOTO_CHECKS`: at most 10 MB, an image we can open, at most `MAX_IMAGE_PIXELS`), and the phone refuses a pick over the cap first (`data-max-bytes`). A refused message keeps its text: the body rides the session (`kiln-unsent-<pk>`) to the redirected page once. On Unload, photos are stored before the firing is locked and removed if the unload is refused or rolls back; a row whose photo was lost to another row's error says to pick it again, and ticking a row back clears its photo. The maker's box is unchanged and has no photo field, so a maker's crafted POST stores the message and no photo; `post_reply` refuses a photo from the ticket's maker too (a crew member on their own ticket is its maker). A ticked (fired) piece's photo on Unload is ignored, even a bad file. `post_reply` and `unload_kiln` write the files before saving the rows and remove them if the save fails (a ticket deleted meanwhile, a rollback). The thread (`kiln/partials/_thread.html`) shows the tile under the message for both sides, alt "Photo from <name>", and a tap opens the full size photo (`hx-boost="false"`, a new tab). The emails never carry the image: `kiln.crew_replied` gets `photo_text` / `photo_block` (blank without a photo), and the ready for pickup notice adds a line linking the ticket under that piece's note and "a note with a photo" to its summary. `KilnTicket.delete_with_photos` deletes the reply photo files after commit with the ticket's own.
- **Replies** (`kiln.services.post_reply`) emit through `core.events`: a crew message is `kiln.crew_replied` to the maker (`SINGLE_USER`, push on by default; reaches class guests); a maker message is `kiln.maker_replied` to the kiln crew (`Recipients.KILN_CREW`, resolver `core.events.resolvers.kiln_crew`; the sender passes it as `recipient_user_ids` minus the author, so a crew member's own ticket does not notify themselves). The settings page shows that row to the kiln crew alone (`_StaffProfile.is_kiln_crew`), so the page equals the send path. Bell, push and email follow each person's switches; the period is the reply, so each message notifies once. No Discord.
- **Unloading** (`kiln.services.unload_kiln`): one transaction locks the firing row first, refuses a firing already unloaded (`FiringAlreadyUnloaded`, the second crew member's toast; nothing written, nothing sent), then locks its tickets. Every ticket is fired unless the crew unticked it (`kiln.forms.UnloadForm` checks each unticked one has what happened, a note and a choice); a back to the queue piece goes to In the queue with its firing cleared and its original `submitted_at`, so it waits at the front and the maker can edit it. The notes are saved as crew replies and do **not** emit `kiln.crew_replied`.
- **Ready for pickup** (`kiln.ready_for_pickup`, `SINGLE_USER` to the maker's account, guests included; bell, push and email on by default): sent after the unload commits, **one per maker per firing** (`period=firing-<pk>` backs that at delivery), listing every piece of theirs and any crew note (`kiln.services.PickupNotice`). One key covers both outcomes, so a maker with a fired piece and a returned piece still gets one notice; the headline says ready, back in the queue, or some of each. The crew member unloading is not told about their own pieces. Links to the ticket when there is one piece, else My Tickets.
- **Deleting** (`kiln.services.delete_ticket`, #1025): at any status (draft, in the queue, in the kiln, ready for pickup), by the maker on their own ticket and by the crew and site admins (`can_run_kiln`) on any; another maker's ticket answers 404 like every page of it, and a GET deletes nothing. It locks the ticket row like loading and unloading, then `KilnTicket.delete_with_photos` removes the ticket with its photos, flags and replies (an unload note goes with it) and deletes the photo files only once the delete commits, like `remove_photo`. The firing is kept (`KilnTicket.firing` and `KilnReply.firing` protect the firing, not the ticket); it simply has one ticket fewer on its page, the Kiln Log count and Unload, and a firing whose tickets were all deleted still unloads. Deleting someone else's ticket needs a note (`kiln.forms.DeleteTicketForm`), and its maker gets one `kiln.ticket_deleted` (`SINGLE_USER`, guests included, bell, push and email on by default, `period=ticket-<pk>`) carrying the note and the piece in words (`KilnTicket.description`: firing, count, clay), linking to My Tickets because the ticket is gone. Nobody is told when a maker deletes their own. Both pages use `kiln/partials/_delete_ticket.html` (the confirm modal, with its note field for the crew). A ticket deleted while Load the Kiln or Unload was open is skipped and counted in the toast (`LoadResult.deleted`, `UnloadResult.deleted`; Unload posts the tickets it `shown`), and a maker saving a ticket deleted meanwhile gets `TicketDeleted` instead of writing it back as a new row.
- Every crew list page keeps its queries flat with nameless people (`tests/kiln/query_counts_spec.py`): Unload, the Unload list, the Kiln Log and a firing's page prefetch `primary_emails` for makers, loaders and unloaders.
- `member_name` is what the pages call a person: the name, or the email when none is on file (guest accounts).
