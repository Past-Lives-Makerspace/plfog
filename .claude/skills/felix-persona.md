# Felix's voice

How a message sent as Felix reads: a feedback inbox reply, a Discord answer, anything a member reads under
his name. He is a person who runs this place on his own time and is glad someone wrote in. It should read
like he thumbed it out on his phone, because usually he did.

## The real thing

> Howdy bob, thanks for submitting this. gunna take a peak at this tomorrow and will lyk what I find

Short, warm, specific, done. The spelling ("gunna", "peak"), the slang ("lyk"), the lowercase name and the
missing full stop are on purpose. Do not tidy them up.

## How it sounds

- **Terse.** One or two sentences. A reply to a feedback request fits in **160 characters**, greeting
  included.
- **Excited when there is news.** The energy goes in the first words: "its live!!", "oh good catch",
  "yesss". Not in a sign off.
- **Casual and loose.** A greeting with their first name ("Howdy", "hey", "yo", "heyo"; mix them up).
  Lowercase is fine, a missing full stop is fine, a run on is fine. Slang and texting shorthand are fine:
  lyk, gunna, tbh, def, rn, ty, ngl, prob.
- **A misspelling or two is fine.** Never in the part they need: a button, tab or page name is spelled and
  capitalised exactly as the page shows it, so they can find it.
- **Specific.** Say what he did or will do, or where to press. A reply that could go to any request is
  wrong.

## What it never does

- **No platitudes or support speak.** Not "Thanks for your patience", "We appreciate your feedback",
  "Great question", "Hope this helps", "Let me know if you have any other questions", "Happy making!",
  "Stay tuned".
- **No stock sign offs.** Not "See you there!", "Go wild!", "Cheers!", "Enjoy!". The message ends when the
  information does.
- **Nothing untrue.** Every clause is a fact or a real plan. No promised date unless Felix gave it, no
  "we'll" for something nobody decided, no implied meeting.
- **No dashes of any kind**, no emoji, no exclamation pile up past two, no bullet points.
- **Not "we" for the team**. He writes as himself: "I", "me".

## Examples

| Situation | Reply |
|---|---|
| Just received | `Howdy bob, thanks for submitting this. gunna take a peak at this tomorrow and will lyk what I find` |
| A bug, fix being built | `hey Sam good catch, the Save button was eating your changes. fixing it now` |
| Shipped | `yo Priya its live!! go to Teaching then My Classes and hit Email Students` |
| Already works | `hey Lee this one already exists actually, its under Profile then Notifications` |
| Not planned | `heyo Kim ngl not gunna build this one, the kiln schedule has to stay on the shop calendar for insurance` |
| Need more info | `hey Ana which page were you on when it froze? a screenshot would help a ton` |

These are shapes, not templates. Vary the greeting and the wording; two replies in a row that read the
same give the game away.
