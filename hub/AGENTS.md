## Home

`views.home` (`hub_home`) renders `hub/home.html` from `hub/home.py::build_home_context`, and answers `Cache-Control: private, no-store` (`_private_no_store`). The Upcoming card opens with a carousel of classes and events (#888, #896; `partials/_home_featured.html`, `static/css/home-featured.css`), then the heading "Upcoming" over the list. `_featured(member)` picks up to `FEATURED_LIMIT` (8) rows of each kind in one query each, shuffled by the database (`_random_order()`, `"?"`, which specs patch to `"pk"` to name the winners): classes from `ClassOffering.objects.featurable()` (open to sign up under `registration_open()`, a seat left, a session still to come; none while the Classes switch is off; instructors prefetched; picture from `ClassOffering.card_photo_url`, the catalog card's rule) and events from `CommunityEvent.objects.featurable_for(member)` (published, not yet begun, visible to the member, a community event or a guild meeting; its photo as the event page crops it, else its flyer, `partials/_event_flyer_sheet.html`, scaled in container units). It keeps `FEATURED_PER_KIND` (4) of each, lets one kind fill the other's place up to eight, and shuffles the two together (`random.shuffle`), so the carousel costs a fixed five queries however many rows exist. The carousel is a `plRotator` (`static/js/pl-rotator.js`, controls in `partials/_rotator_controls.html`): one slide at a time, a 400ms cross fade every 6 seconds, dots, a time bar, arrows, a pause button and swipe; it holds while hovered, focused, paused or in a hidden tab, never moves by itself under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and shows no controls with one slide. With nothing to show the card is the heading and the list.

Above everything on Home, for every viewer of Home with a membership or without one, sits the announcements banner (#889, `partials/_home_announcements.html`, `static/css/home-announcements.css`): `HomeBanner.load(now)` in `hub/home.py`, one query for `Announcement.objects.live(now)`, None while nothing is live. With two or more it is a second `plRotator` with its own scope and clock (8 seconds, the same controls, holds and reduced-motion rule as the carousel); with one there is no control and no timer. Its X keeps `HomeBanner.signature` in localStorage (`pl-home-announcements-closed`) and removes the banner; an inline script after it removes it again on every load until the live set changes (a new, edited, begun or ended announcement; a new order alone does not). Every storage call is in a try, so with storage blocked the banner simply shows.

## List / Calendar pages

The Orientations and Reservations pages (#502) open on List and build their calendar only for `?view=calendar`; otherwise the Calendar pane fetches the shell once on first open (`static/js/list_calendar.js`, any placeholder carrying `data-lazy-pane` and `data-pane-src`). Both reuse the guild page's shell (`partials/guild_calendar_app.html`) with `cal_key` and `cal.legend`; `hub/calendar_pages.py` builds their context on `hub/calendar_window.py` (`calendar_window_context`), the one place every calendar's date arithmetic lives, from rows in `hub/calendar_entries.py`.

The Orientations page's Bookings tab (#626, `?view=bookings`) loads the same way. Its rows are scoped by `membership.permissions.manageable_orientation_bookings` (the queryset form of `_require_can_manage_booking`) plus the viewer's own; a member who runs nothing sees only their own. The Reservations page's Bookings tab (#627) is its twin, scoped by `membership.permissions.manageable_reservations` (the queryset form of `can_manage_equipment`); its staff view adds a Needs Approval chip, with a count, while requests on equipment the viewer manages wait for approval (#748), and Approve and Decline Reservation in a waiting row's menu post to `hub_equipment_reservation_approve` and `hub_equipment_reservation_decline`, the same endpoints as the manage page's Needs Approval card. Both lists pass `honour_preview=True`, so an admin previewing as a member sees what that member would; action gates never pass it. Shared pieces live in `hub/bookings_tab.py` and `static/css/bookings-tab.css`.

## Email Members (the sending feature)

Called Announcements until #920; code names (`AnnouncementDraft`, `hub_compose*`, `hub_announcements`, `_announcement_rows`) kept theirs. The Admin Tools card carries a "Formerly called Announcements" pill (one line in `hub/admin_tools.html`), and Manage Mailing Lists (`tool_mailing_lists`) sits beside it. Guild Settings' tab reads Email Members; its key stays `announcements`. The composer (`hub_compose*`) and the Email Members page share one visibility rule, `_announcement_rows(request, member)` in `hub/views.py`: a row is visible and actionable exactly when `_compose_audience_forbidden(request, draft.audience_value)` is `None` (admins short-circuit to every row). Every composer lookup that takes a `draft_pk` goes through `_handled_draft`, which applies it to resumable rows only. Drafts are shared; `author` is whoever saved last, and the sender once sent.

Guild leads (`leads`) and All Instructors (`instructor`, #806) are admin only audiences sent in the request through `emit("site_announcement")` with an explicit recipient set, so each person's makerspace announcement preferences apply unless the send is urgent. All Instructors is `Member.objects.instructors()`, signed in or not; an instructor with no account is emailed at the address on file only while Email is on, and the composer's count (`instructor_reach`) moves with the Email switch.

On a guild or class announcement the recipient checklist carries "Add people" (#729): a searchable list of members not yet listed with one "Add selected" (`static/js/compose_add_people.js`, the `composeAddPeople` component), and an "Add email addresses" box vetted by `hub_compose_add_addresses` (`classify_announcement_additions`). A guild send saves everyone added to the guild's `GuildMailingListEmail` list (`Guild.remember_announcement_recipients`, in `AnnouncementDraft.send`); a row with a `user` is a saved member, listed by name and reached as a member (`Guild.mailing_list_users`), removed in Guild Settings. A class send saves nothing, and a draft save never does.

## The Spotlight (#709)

The top left of every hub page for members. `hub_sidebar` adds a lazy `spotlight` (`hub.spotlight.Spotlight.load(now)`, two queries: the settings row with its meeting, then `core.Announcement.objects.live(now)`), None for guests (#691), former members and signed-out visitors (`hub.spotlight.sees_spotlight`, the polls app's old `can_vote` rule moved here in #890), who keep the version number and the plain changelog modal. Every member sees the same Spotlight. `partials/_spotlight.html` renders Standard and Minimized; `<html data-spotlight-min>` picks one in CSS, set before first paint by an inline head script and kept by `static/js/spotlight.js` (an Alpine store, in the head before Alpine), which also keeps the seen signature (`Spotlight.seen_signature`) in localStorage and shows the yellow dot on Minimized when it differs.

Standard since #890, top to bottom: the live announcements (`partials/_spotlight_announcements.html`: eyebrow "Spotlight Message" (named Announcement until #920), each one's `body_html`; with two or more a `plRotator` timed like the Home banner, 8 seconds, with previous, "2 of 3", next and pause in one row from `partials/_rotator_controls.html` with `counter=True`), the Next Feature Request Meeting with its Virtual pill (the rotator's pause lasts until play or logging out, per user and per rotator: FRONTEND.md, `rotator.css` row), then the foot: the version pill, "Updated X" and Details (`partials/_spotlight_quiet.html`). Polls were removed in #890 (`polls/AGENTS.md`: only the bare models stay until their drop migration). Minimized's first line is the admin's text, else the first announcement's words on one line, else the latest update's title. The seen signature is the announcements' ids and last saves (`Announcement.signature`, the Home banner's), the next meeting date and the text stamp, so a new or edited announcement lights the dot, a new order alone does not, and a new release never does.

Details opens `components/modal.html` "spotlight-expanded" with `partials/_spotlight_expanded.html`: every live announcement in full in a Spotlight Messages section, or with none the newest changelog entry (`LatestUpdate`, `partials/_spotlight_update.html`, which opens the changelog at it); the meeting; Suggest a feature / Report a bug (`?category=`); and "Version X.Y · What changed", which shows `includes/_changelog_pages.html` (five a page; the plain modal on public, login and admin pages uses it too). A `#changelog-<slug>` link opens the panel on that entry's page. With no announcement and no meeting the Spotlight is Quiet (the foot alone) and shows only while `SiteConfiguration.spotlight_show_when_empty` is on; off, `Spotlight.__bool__` is False and the corner goes back to the logo and version number. `hub.context_processors.request_spotlight` reads it once per request, and Home's banner (`hub.home.HomeBanner.for_request`) reuses its announcements whenever the page carries a Spotlight (`shows_spotlight`), so Home reads them once. Home repeats the Spotlight at the top for phones (`.pl-spotlight-home`, hidden over 768px) as `spotlight.without_announcements`, since the banner just above shows them; it keeps the full seen signature, so opening it records them as seen.

## Common Pattern

All views call `_get_hub_context(request)` for sidebar data (guild list, user initials) and `_get_member(request)` to get the logged-in `Member`. Views gracefully handle `member is None` (unlinked account).

## Forms (hub/forms.py)

- `VotePreferenceForm` — 3 guild FK selects (guild_1st, guild_2nd, guild_3rd)
- `ProfileSettingsForm` — edits Member fields (pronouns, about_me, discord_handle, etc.)
- `EmailPreferencesForm` — email notification toggles
- `BetaFeedbackForm` — bug/feature/general feedback; `form.submit(user=user)` saves a `core.models.FeedbackRequest`, then emails the admins with a link to it
- `FeedbackRequestAdminForm` — the inbox's status, note and GitHub link (a plain Form, so the instance keeps its saved values until `apply_admin_update`)
- `SiteSettingsForm`, `MemberAgreementSettingsForm`, `ManageFeaturesForm`, `ManageCmsForm`, `AutomationsForm` — one page's columns of `SiteConfiguration` each (#922); a field belongs to exactly one, because a checkbox a page does not render reads as off on its save. The four slices save with `update_fields`, so one page never puts back what another page just changed
- Self-service tab-entry form: `billing.forms.TabItemForm` with `context="member_tab_page"` (replaces the old `AddTabEntryForm` removed in v1.5.0)
